# HyFib Tailor 360

Production-grade tailoring operations platform: customer intake and measurements, configurable stitching
categories (Blouse — Pattern and Aari work, Salwar, Lehenga, Gown, Kids), shape and design selection, material and
reference images, multi-garment orders with barcode chain-of-custody, inventory with an immutable stock ledger and
low-stock alerts, GST billing and payments, reports, delivery and customer feedback — delivered as an installable
PWA for Android and iOS phones and tablets and desktop browsers, backed by an ASP.NET Core modular monolith on
PostgreSQL.

- Roadmap and backlog: [issue #1](https://github.com/MK-AIFy/HyFib-Tailor360/issues/1) and its epics.
