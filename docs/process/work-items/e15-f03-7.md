Sub-issue of #61. The closing deliverable of the plan's **#61b**: the parent's second required verification,
"production-like dress rehearsal including restore and rollback". Two documents already promise it and neither can
be satisfied today: `docs/process/release-evidence.md` line 22 and `docs/process/release-gates.md` line 20 both say
the release evidence form "applies to every tagged release deployed to production, **and to the release rehearsals
of #59, #60 and #61b**", and no rehearsal record of any kind exists — `ls docs/qa` fails before child [E15-F03-1]
merges, and there is no `docs/runbooks` directory yet either (it arrives with #60). This sub-issue runs the
rehearsal, writes the procedure that makes it repeatable, and files the completed record.

## What is already built (do not re-build)

  9 when the candidate is cut, a named person confirms each row, an item that does not apply is marked `n/a` with a
  reason and never deleted, an unfinished row blocks the release, and `RG-14` carries no waiver. **The rehearsal
  fills in a copy of this form. Do not write a second form.**

## What is missing

- No procedure ties #59's deploy and rollback to #60's restore and to the regression suite in one rehearsal, so no
  release has ever been shown to be survivable end to end.
- No completed evidence form exists for any build, which means `RG-14` has never been exercised and the form has
  never been tested against a real run.
- Nothing records what the rehearsal measured: the interruption window, the recovery point, the recovery time, and
  which gate would have blocked.

## Scope

      Stages: cut the candidate and verify its signature and provenance (`RG-11`); provision a clean environment

      the restored environment; complete the release evidence form.
- [ ] Each stage names the person who confirms it, using the gate owners of `docs/process/release-gates.md` section

## Out of scope

- Building, changing or hardening any deployment, backup, restore or rollback mechanism. All #59 and #60. A
  mechanism that cannot be rehearsed is a defect raised against its owning issue.
- Writing a disaster-recovery runbook. `docs/runbooks/` is #60's; the rehearsal executes those runbooks and reports
  where they were unclear.
- The pilot on real devices, the user-acceptance run and the go/no-go decision — children [E15-F03-8],
  [E15-F03-8b], [E15-F03-9], [E15-F03-10] and [E15-F03-11].
- Any production deployment. The rehearsal runs on the production-**like** environment; the go-live release itself is
  decided by [E15-F03-11] and executed by #59's workflow.
- Changing a published recovery objective. `docs/nfr/slo.md` section 6 owns `RPO` and `RTO`; the rehearsal reports
  the measurement beside the target and, if the target is missed, that is a finding with an owner, not a new target.

## Migration and rollback

No migration is authored. Migrations are **executed and deliberately broken** as part of the rehearsal: the run
applies every outstanding migration to the provisioned environment, then induces a failure and rolls back to the
previous tag with the new tables unused — the shape `docs/dev/migrations.md` prescribes. The record carries the
`migrate --dry-run` output, the `migrate` output, the failure, the rollback and the post-rollback health check. The
rollback of **this pull request** is `git revert` of two documents.
