## What is missing

### The four document fields the payload does not expose

`InvoicePayload.Calculation` is `InvoiceCalculationPayload(Reference, PriceListVersionId,
TaxConfigurationVersionId, GstRegistrationId, Gstin, SupplierStateCode, PlaceOfSupplyStateCode, Scheme,
TaxInclusive)`. The document model's `supplier` section is `{ legalName, tradeName, gstin, stateCode, branchName }`,
and the model also carries `terms`. So of the six the printed document shows, the client can read only **`gstin`**
and **`stateCode`**. The other four:

| Field | Where the document gets it | What this slice does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `supplier.legalName` | `invoice.Calculation.SupplierLegalName` — **frozen on the invoice**, already on the domain record (`InvoiceValues.cs`), simply not projected | **Project it.** One additive field on `InvoiceCalculationPayload` |
| `supplier.tradeName` | `invoice.Calculation.SupplierTradeName`, nullable, same place | **Project it.** One additive nullable field |
| `supplier.branchName` | A branch-register read inside `DocumentArtifactHandler` (`branch?.Name ?? branch?.Code`), not on the invoice at all | **Out of scope.** The view does not show a branch name. Putting one on the invoice payload would mean either a branch read from a screen whose caller holds no admin permission, or a new denormalised column — a Billing design change, not a client slice |
| `terms` | `Billing:Documents:Terms` configuration, read by the worker at render time | **Out of scope.** Configuration, not invoice data. The view says in words that the PDF is the artefact of record and carries the letterhead and the terms |

**A view that silently omitted the seller's legal name would be a GST invoice preview with no seller on it**, which
is why the two projections are in scope rather than deferred. They are the only server change in this slice.

- **A binary read path.** `readBody` returns `undefined` for a PDF, and `send` asks for `application/json`, so
  `apiRequest<Blob>` silently yields nothing. No `apiRequestBlob` exists.
- **The in-app print view.** Nothing renders the invoice in document form on screen; there is no `@media print`
  block anywhere under `clients/pwa/src/routes/billing/`.
- **The three document controls.** No client caller for download, for the note's document, or for the print queue.
