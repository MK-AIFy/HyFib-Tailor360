## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Tailor Master opens the route for a garment in production, sees the checklist's **code and version number**
      and its criteria as named groups, marks every criterion a pass and submits; the screen then shows the recorded
      result, read back from the server and not assumed (client guide section 6: "the client never asserts what the
      server has not confirmed").
- [ ] Failing a criterion requires at least one defect code, chosen **by name**, allows more than one, and lets the
      selection be re-read before submitting; after submitting, the screen offers opening a rework and announces the
      phase the garment returns to.
- [ ] Opening and completing a rework both work from the same route, and the QC history afterwards shows the pass,
      the failure and the later result as separate entries with their own timestamps.
- [ ] Accessibility: axe clean at the phone, tablet and desktop profiles, at 200% zoom and at 320 px;
      `expectNoHorizontalOverflow` green with its **default** five widths at both zoom levels; the submit control
      meets the 56 × 56 CSS px primary shop-floor target of client guide section 4 (**AL-03**, proposed — see Open
      decisions); every field has a persistent visible label; the result announcement goes through a polite live
      region; nothing auto-advances.
- [ ] Localisation: no hard-coded English, both `ordersEn` and `ordersTa` complete (enforced by `pnpm typecheck`),
      the layout intact at the pseudo-locale's 40% growth and at the 150% product text size, and every date, time
      and measurement rendered through the shared formatters.
- [ ] **Negative**: a caller without `orders.record_qc` sees the `admin.forbidden.title`/`admin.forbidden.body`
      `EmptyState` and no form — asserted by the forbidden test; a `403` or `409` from the server renders as plain
      language through the problem alert and never as a code; a failed submit leaves the typed input in place and a
      retry reuses the **same** `Idempotency-Key`; offline, the submit shows `OfflineBlockedAction` and is not
      queued.
- [ ] **Exception**: a garment with no pinned checklist version renders the empty state explaining that the service
      type has no published checklist, not an error; a garment that never entered production renders the server's
      refusal in plain language; a result already recorded is shown as immutable with no edit control anywhere on
      the screen.
