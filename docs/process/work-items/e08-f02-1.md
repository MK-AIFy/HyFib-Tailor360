### The fold, as this child implements it

| Entry type | Moves | Sign allowed | Read it as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Opening` | `on_hand` | positive | The stock that existed before the ledger did |
| `PurchaseReceipt` | `on_hand` | positive | Arrival against a receipt line (T5) |
| `Reservation` | `reserved` | positive | A claim on available stock (T1) |
| `Release` | `reserved` | negative | The claim given up |
| `Issue` | `on_hand` **always**; `reserved` **only when the posting names a reservation**, and only by that reservation's held quantity | negative | Material leaves the location for a garment job; the claim it realises falls with it (T2) |
| `Consumption` | nothing | negative | What a job used of what it was issued (T3). The material left `on_hand` at the issue; counting it twice is what the fold exists to prevent |
| `Return` | `on_hand` | positive | Unused material back on the shelf |
| `TransferOut` | `on_hand` | negative | The source leg of a balanced pair (T6) |
| `TransferIn` | `on_hand` | positive | The destination leg of the same pair |
| `Wastage` | `on_hand` | negative | Spoiled or lost, with a reason (T4) |
| `Adjustment` | `on_hand` | either | A stocktake posting or a compensating correction |

**`reserved` is not a function of the entry type alone, and that is the one place this table is easy to implement
wrongly.** Read the `Issue` row literally — "moves `on_hand` and `reserved`" — and an issue with no reservation behind
it drives `reserved` below zero, which this child's own guard then refuses, so the commonest movement in the shop
would be rejected. So the fold is written as two rules, not one table lookup:

1. **`on_hand`** is a pure function of the entry type and the sign, exactly as the table says.
2. **`reserved`** moves on three postings only: up on a `Reservation`; down on a `Release`; and down on an `Issue`
   **that names a reservation**, by **that reservation's whole held quantity** — not by the quantity issued.
   `Consumption`, `Return`, `Wastage`, `Adjustment`, `TransferOut`, `TransferIn` and `Opening` never move it.

   The "whole held quantity" half is the other thing a session would otherwise have to guess, so fix it here: an
   `Issue` that names a reservation **resolves that reservation in full**, whatever it issues. Reserve 10 and issue 4
   against it, and `on_hand` falls by 4, `reserved` falls by 10, the reservation ends `Consumed`, and the other 6
   become available to anyone again — the claim was given up by issuing against it. There is therefore **no partial
   reservation state, no residual hold and no second draw-down**, which is what keeps INV-STK-05 ("a reservation is
   always resolved") true with a three-state machine. A shop that wants the remaining 6 held reserves again.

The arithmetic that makes this the right reading: reserve 10 of 100 leaves `on_hand` 100, `reserved` 10 and
`available` 90. Issue those 10 against the reservation and `on_hand` is 90, `reserved` 0, `available` 90 — unchanged,
which is correct, because the reserved stock was never available. Issue 5 with no reservation and `available` falls by
5, which is also correct. Any other reading double-counts or goes negative.

**One rule about when a reservation resolves, and it is this child's job to write it down.** A reservation resolves
`Held → Consumed` at the **`Issue` that names it** — the moment the claim is realised and `reserved` falls. A
`Consumption` entry never touches a reservation, never moves a balance component, and cannot resolve anything. Stated
once here so that `E08-F02-2` (the state machine) and `E08-F02-3` (the command) cannot implement two different rules.

That reading contradicts two cells of `docs/architecture/sequences/stock-reservation.md` section 4 as it stands today,
and **amending exactly those two cells is in scope**:

- T3's *Contains* cell reads "One `consumption` ledger entry, the reservation drawn down, the updated balance, audit
  and outbox". "The reservation drawn down" belongs to T2, and "the updated balance" becomes "no balance component
  moves — the material left `on_hand` at the issue".
- T2's *Contains* cell gains the reservation draw-down it actually performs.

Add the fold table and both amendments under that file's own section 10 maintenance rule. If a reviewer rejects this
reading, `E08-F02-2`, `E08-F02-3` and `E08-F02-6` move with it — which is why this child is reviewed first.
