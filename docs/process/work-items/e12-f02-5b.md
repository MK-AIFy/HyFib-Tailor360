Sub-issue of #51. #E12-F02-5a says *that* something is waiting, on every screen. This sub-issue is where a person goes
to do something about it: the `/sync` screen with its three sections, the start-up notice for a queue the browser
evicted, and the gate on the deliberate sign-out. It is the last visible piece of the offline queue, and it is the one
that answers the parent's "sync state, pending count, conflicts and **recovery actions** visible" in full.

## What is missing

- There is no `clients/pwa/src/routes/offline/` directory and no `/sync` path in the router.
- Signing out with queued work is possible and silent.
- Nothing reads `queueMirror` at start-up, so an evicted queue is simply gone with no account of what was lost.
