Sub-issue of #40. `E08-F03-5` computes variances, takes the explanation, refuses self-approval and posts adjustment
ledger entries; none of it is reachable from a device, and the two acceptance criteria it carries for #40 — "users
cannot approve their own material variance above configured threshold" and "stocktake never rewrites historical ledger
entries" — are the two a person most needs to *see* working. This child is the variance screen and the posting action,
the slice `docs/nfr/a11y-checklist.md` `A11Y-RJ-05` steps 6 and 7 grade. It is separate from `E08-F03-9` because it is
a **different actor** (the approver, who is not the counter) with a step-up flow, and because the two together would
not fit one pull request: `clients/pwa/src/routes/billing/CashierSessionRoute.tsx` alone is 440 production lines for
one comparable screen.

## The offline story, because this is where a reviewer and the tree disagree

`docs/process/definition-of-done.md` **DoD 7** asks, word for word, that "Storybook stories exist for the loading,
empty, error, **offline** and forbidden state of every new screen", and `clients/pwa/CLAUDE.md` section 6 repeats it.
But `clients/pwa/src/routes/catalog/catalogScreens.stories.tsx` ships **no** offline story and records why in its own
doc comment: "editing and publishing a catalogue are online-only acts, and the application says so through
`NetworkStatusBanner` and `OfflineBlockedAction`, which carry their own stories. An offline story here would imply
these screens queue what a person did, and they must not."

Read that exception for what it is: it is about a screen whose offline story would **imply queueing**. These are
**operational** screens whose offline state is a *visible refusal* — `OfflineBlockedAction` saying the action will not
be queued — which is exactly what **NFR-CU-05** requires of inventory actions and what a person on a shop floor needs
to see. So: **write the offline story**, and put one sentence in the story file's doc comment saying that it renders
the refusal rather than a queue, so it reads as following the catalogue's reasoning rather than ignoring it. Do **not**
produce an offline *screenshot* at five widths; the offline state is a story and a Vitest assertion, not a device
photograph.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A counter holding `inventory.stocktake` explains both variances on a closed session; a **different** user
      holding `inventory.approve_variance` approves both with a reason and step-up; the counter then posts and sees a
      summary naming the two adjustment entries written — one Vitest journey through `withAdminApi` with two stubbed
      users, asserting the request bodies and headers.
- [ ] **Negative (the criterion this screen exists to make visible):** when the approver and the counter are the same
      person, the server's `403` same-user refusal is rendered in words that say a second person must approve — proved
      by a story and a test, not by inspection. Note that this is a **different** `403` from
      `matrix.yaml`'s `not-permitted` (`security.forbidden`), from `second-factor`
      (`security.second-factor-required`) and from `step-up` (`security.step-up-required`): four refusals share a
      status code and each needs its own words, which is exactly the class of defect #99 fixed.
- [ ] **Negative:** with a variance beyond the threshold still pending, the post action is disabled with a visible
      explanation naming the waiting lines, and a forced attempt renders the server's `409` as words.
- [ ] **Negative:** a caller holding neither key sees the `RequirePermission` empty state (`admin.forbidden.title` /
      `admin.forbidden.body`) and is not redirected; a caller holding `inventory.stocktake` only sees the sheet and
      the explain control with **no** approval control anywhere in the DOM.
- [ ] **Exception:** a declined or cancelled step-up renewal leaves the screen usable, says what was needed, and the
      retry that follows carries the **same** `Idempotency-Key` — the behaviour #99 fixed for every other approval
      screen. Asserted **twice**: once on the approval read, where an Owner meets the demand on arrival, and once on
      the approve command.
- [ ] **Exception:** an Owner holding **only** `inventory.approve_variance` reaches the sheet, reads it and approves
      from it — no `403` on the read, and no empty screen. This is #220's first acceptance criterion transposed to
      Inventory, and the single most valuable test in this child.
- [ ] **Exception/offline:** with the network down the sheet reads from its last value and every write renders
      `OfflineBlockedAction` saying it will not be queued (**NFR-CU-05** in `docs/nfr/traceability.md`).
- [ ] Accessibility: `expectNoAccessibilityViolations` on every state; each variance is announced as a signed pair
      with its units (`A11Y-RJ-05` step 6); the posting dialogue announces what is approved and that it is
      irreversible (step 7); focus returns to the row the dialogue came from when it closes.
