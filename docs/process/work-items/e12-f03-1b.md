## Which journeys are actually automatable at HEAD — read this before writing a spec

This is the fact the whole parent turns on, and it was got wrong once already. `init-reference-data` seeds the
organisation, roles, grants, consent purposes, the initial catalogue, design groups, measurement templates and
payment modes; `seed-synthetic` writes two branches, one feature flag and **[E12-F03-1a]**'s staff accounts. There
is **no customer, no order, no invoice and no stock row**, and there is no client route that can create a customer
(`router.tsx` has no customers route — that is #182).

So:

- **Completable**: sign-in without a second factor; sign-in with one; the not-found screen; `install`; `about`;
  `settings/display`; `account/security` and `account/sessions`; `admin/users` (it lists the seeded accounts) →
  `admin/users/:userId` → suspend through `ConfirmDialog` → reactivate; `admin/branches` (it lists `MAIN` and
  `SECOND`, and `BranchListRoute` line 175 has a `ConfirmDialog`); `admin/roles`, `admin/templates`,
  `admin/catalog`, `admin/features`, `admin/audit` and `admin/outbox` as reads.
- **Reachable but only as an empty or error state**: `measurements` and `measurements/new`
  (`MeasurementStartRoute` line 62 reads `?customerId=` and otherwise shows a customer search that returns
  nothing); `billing/outstanding`; `billing/payments/new` (`TakePaymentRoute` line 47 reads `?orderId=` and calls
  `getOrderBalance(orderId)` — with no order there is no balance and the journey cannot be completed).
- **Not automatable at all yet**: every journey that needs a customer, an order, an invoice, a label, a scan or a
  stock row. A step whose data or screen does not exist is **not** stubbed, skipped or driven through the API as a
  substitute: the journey is not automatable, and the honest outcome is that its spec arrives with the issue that
  builds it.
