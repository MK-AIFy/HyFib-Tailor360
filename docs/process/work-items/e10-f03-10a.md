## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An Owner opens `/reports/profitability`, filters to one month and one category, reads the margin with its
      components, and opens the method-and-assumptions panel, which names the assumption version, the valuation method
      and run, the configuration versions, and every unavailable component — against stubbed transport in the route
      test and over the real API in the evidence.
- [ ] A figure computed with **no published assumption version** is shown labelled "estimated — incomplete" with
      labour and overhead listed as unavailable **and the reason in words**; the test asserts the label is the
      **stored** one rather than one the screen decided, and the screen does **not** show a zero for a missing
      component.
- [ ] The screen shows all five states: loading, empty (no order or job matches the filter, with the filter named),
      error (an `ApiProblem` through the shared alert, **never** the raw message), offline (`NetworkStatusBanner`, the
      export action blocked rather than queued), and forbidden (`RequirePermission`'s `EmptyState` with
      `admin.forbidden.title` / `admin.forbidden.body` — **not** the `Forbidden` component, and **no** redirect).
- [ ] **Negative:** a caller holding `reports.read` without the margin gate sees the completeness and identifying
      columns with **every money column absent** and the explanation line announced; the test asserts the header is
      absent rather than blank or zero.
- [ ] **Negative:** opening a garment job's row that belongs to a branch the caller is not assigned to is answered
      `404 security.resource-not-found` by the server (`e10-f03-6`'s resource scope) and rendered as "not found or not
      yours" — the two are indistinguishable by design, and the screen must not say which.
- [ ] **Exception:** a **cancelled** garment job's row is shown with its costs, no revenue and its exclusion flag, per
      the metric dictionary — neither hidden nor silently counted in a margin total; and a trend range above the
      synchronous limit shows the asynchronous offer from `reporting.range-too-large` through the shared export
      action, not an error.
- [ ] `A11Y-DB-01`, `-02`, `-03`, `-07`, `-08` and `-09` pass; `expectNoHorizontalOverflow` passes at 320, 360, 768,
      1024 and 1280 px at 100% and 200%; `pnpm --dir clients/pwa lint`, `typecheck`, `test`, `build` and
      `format:check` are clean, and `generate:api:check` reports no drift.
