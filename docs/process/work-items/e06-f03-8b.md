Sub-issue of #34, and the second of two exception-dashboard client slices. **E06-F03-8a builds the route and the five
read-only views; this slice adds the four things a Branch Manager actually does from them.** It is the client half of
#34's third acceptance criterion, "Hold/cancel/alteration actions enforce permissions and downstream reconciliation",
and the desk half of its "UAT for QC failure, alteration and cancellation scenarios".

Each action is a command that already exists on the server by the time this merges: resume a hold and reschedule a
garment (E06-F03-3), cancel a garment and cancel an order (E06-F03-3), and accept or reject an alteration
(E06-F03-4b). **All five of those permissions carry Reason = yes in `docs/security/permission-matrix.md`** (lines
220, 222, 223, 233, 234), so every dialogue has a mandatory reason field, and every command takes an `If-Match` and an
`Idempotency-Key`.

## Scope

- [ ] Five row actions, each in its own component file under `clients/pwa/src/routes/orders/` so no panel grows a
      dialogue inline — `ResumeHoldDialog.tsx`, `RescheduleDialog.tsx`, `CancelGarmentDialog.tsx`,
      `CancelOrderDialog.tsx`, `AlterationDecisionDialog.tsx` — each:
      gated by its own permission through `ORDERS_PERMISSIONS` (`orders.resume`, `orders.reschedule`,
      `orders.cancel_job`, `orders.cancel`, `orders.alteration_decide` — never a string literal at a call site);
      carrying a **mandatory reason** field, because all five permissions carry Reason = yes;
      sending `If-Match` from the `ETag` the row's read returned and a fresh `Idempotency-Key` per attempt, **reused
      unchanged on a retry**;
      and reading the result back from the server rather than assuming it (client guide section 6: "the client never
      asserts what the server has not confirmed").
- [ ] The alteration decision dialogue additionally takes the **outcome** (accept or **reject**), the charge intent
      (chargeable with an amount, or complimentary) and the new promised date; a rejection takes its reason and no
      charge. Amounts go through the shared formatters; no arithmetic happens in the client.
- [ ] Destructive-action discipline (client guide section 4): the two cancel actions use the appropriate tier of
      `confirmTiers.ts`, are **not** placed adjacent to the frequent actions, and no action sits in the bottom 8 CSS
      px of a phone viewport. The reschedule dialogue's date control respects `DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE` and offers no date
      before today.
- [ ] **Refusals rendered as meaning, not as codes**, from `ordersProblems.ts`: the cancellation guard's named arm —
      financial, custody-not-evaluated, or stock-not-evaluated — is rendered in plain language, so a Branch Manager
      reads "the order has an unpaid balance of …; take the payment or raise a credit note" and, where an arm was not
      evaluated, "the stock position was not checked" rather than silence. The raw `failure` is passed to the alert
      and **its message is never rendered**. A `412` reads "this changed while you were reading it" with a reload
      control, never as a code. A `409` from a refused transition reads in the garment's own terms.
- [ ] Extend the `clients/pwa/src/orders/` feature folder — `ordersApi.ts`, `ordersPermissions.ts`,
      `ordersProblems.ts`, `types.ts`, `testing/fixtures.ts` (synthetic fixtures only) — and
      `clients/pwa/src/i18n/messages/orders.ts` in **both** languages — a key in one
      language and not the other fails `pnpm typecheck` (`i18n/en-IN.ts`).
- [ ] Extend `clients/pwa/src/routes/orders/exceptionDashboardScreens.stories.tsx` with one story per dialogue,
      through `withAdminApi` and `StoryRoutes`, including a refused-cancellation story driven by `storyProblem` and a
      pending story driven by `storyPending`. **No offline story** — `catalogScreens.stories.tsx`'s header records why
      admin screens have none; the **blocked action carries its own story** instead, which is the one new story kind
      this slice adds over 8a.
- [ ] Vitest tests beside the subject, querying by role, label and text.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] From the overdue-holds tab a Branch Manager resumes a hold with a reason and the row leaves the tab on the next
      read; from the same tab a garment is rescheduled to a new promised date with a reason.
- [ ] From the pending-alterations tab an alteration is **accepted** with a charge intent and a new promised date, and
      another is **rejected** with a reason; both results are read back from the server and the rejected one is shown
      as rejected rather than disappearing.
- [ ] From the failed-QC and cancelled-jobs tabs a garment is cancelled, and an order is cancelled, each behind the
      appropriate `confirmTiers.ts` tier with a mandatory reason; each result is read back from the server.
- [ ] Accessibility: axe clean at the phone, tablet and desktop profiles, at 200% zoom and at 320 px;
      `expectNoHorizontalOverflow` green at its **default** five widths at both zoom levels; every dialogue
      keyboard-operable with focus trapped and returned to the invoking row on close; focus never obscured by a sticky
      bar; the cancel confirmations at the 56 × 56 CSS px primary target and standard controls at 44 × 44 (**AL-03**,
      proposed — see Open decisions); each outcome announced through a polite live region; nothing auto-advances.
- [ ] Localisation: no hard-coded English, both languages complete (enforced by `pnpm typecheck`), the layout intact
      at the pseudo-locale's 40% growth and the 150% product text size, and every date, age and amount through the
      shared formatters.
- [ ] **Negative**: a caller holding `orders.read` but not `orders.cancel_job` sees the rows and **no** cancel action,
      **and** the same caller calling the endpoint anyway is refused by the server — both asserted, because the client
      gate is decoration; the same pair for `orders.reschedule`, which matrix line 61 records as the Branch Manager's
      **sole** act, so the action is hidden for every other role including the Tailor Master; a refused cancellation
      renders the guard's named arm in plain language; offline, every action shows `OfflineBlockedAction` and nothing
      is queued; a failed command keeps the typed reason and a retry reuses the **same** `Idempotency-Key`; a `412`
      from a stale `If-Match` is rendered as a reload prompt and never as a code.
- [ ] **Negative**: the alteration decision action is hidden for `tailor_master`. `docs/prd/state-transitions.md`
      line 230 says "Branch Manager or Tailor Master" while `docs/security/permission-matrix.md` line 220 and
      `SystemRoles.cs:198` grant `orders.alteration_decide` to `branch_manager` alone; **the matrix wins**, as
      E06-F03-4b records, and the disagreement is registered under OD-13 rather than resolved in the client.
- [ ] **Exception**: a dialogue opened on a row that has since changed shows the server's `412` and reloads the tab
      rather than retrying blindly; a dialogue whose row has been cancelled by someone else shows the server's `409`
      in the garment's own terms; closing a dialogue mid-typing discards the reason and does not persist it anywhere.

## Data and classification

| Item | Class | Purpose | Retention | Who may read it | Logs, telemetry, export |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The reason typed into a dialogue | Internal, free text — required by every one of these five permissions | The record of why a hold was lifted, a date moved, a garment cancelled or an alteration decided | Held in component state until the command returns, then discarded | The caller | **Never** in client telemetry and never in a retry log line. The server records it in the audit row, which is where a reason belongs |
| The order balance and the named arm in a refusal | **Confidential** — `docs/nfr/data-classification.md` section 5.7 classes the price snapshot Confidential and forbids it in logs | Tell the Branch Manager what blocks the cancellation | Not stored client-side | Callers whose response view includes money | Never in telemetry |
| The alteration charge intent and the promised date typed into the decision dialogue | **Confidential** (the amount) and Internal (the date) | Commit the decision's terms | Not stored client-side; sent once | Callers holding `orders.alteration_decide` | Never in telemetry |
| The `Idempotency-Key` and the `ETag` held across a retry | Internal, opaque | Make a retry safe | Component state for the life of the attempt | The caller | Never logged. They are not identifiers of anything personal, and they are not telemetry attributes either |
| The customer's name on a row a dialogue opens from | **Personal** — section 5.7 | Name the person whose garment is being cancelled, so the wrong one is not | Not stored client-side | Callers whose server response includes it | Never in telemetry and never in a log |

No new credential, no secret, no export, no new media class, **nothing written to `localStorage`** (a shop device is
shared — client guide section 6), and no image fetched.
