Sub-issue of #34, and the first of two exception-dashboard client slices. The parent's seventh implementation step —
"Provide exception dashboards for failed QC, repeated rework, overdue holds, pending alterations and cancelled jobs" —
and its acceptance criterion "Exception dashboards reconcile with job history" need a screen, and there is none.
`clients/pwa/src/routes/` holds `admin/`, `auth/`, `billing/`, `catalog/` and `measurements/` and **no orders
routes**; `clients/pwa/src/` has `admin/`, `auth/`, `billing/`, `catalog/`, `customers/`, `measurements/` and **no
`orders/`**; and the Branch Manager's view of a stuck shop exists only as the mock
`clients/pwa/src/stories/journeys/TailorMasterWorkboard.tsx` over `clients/pwa/src/stories/fixtures/production.ts`.

**This slice builds the route and the five read-only views. The row actions are E06-F03-8b.** They are split because
one session cannot carry five tabbed tables and five permission-gated, reason-mandatory, `If-Match`-carrying
dialogues: the merged calibration is `clients/pwa/src/routes/catalog/CatalogVersionEditorRoute.tsx` at **978
production lines for one route**, before its entry components, its API module, its messages and its stories.

## Out of scope

**Every action.** Resume, reschedule, cancel a garment, cancel an order and decide an alteration are **E06-F03-8b**;
this slice renders five read-only tables and offers no command, which is why it needs no dialogue, no `If-Match`, no
`Idempotency-Key` and no `OfflineBlockedAction`. The five reads themselves — E06-F03-6. Recording a QC result and the
rework panel — E06-F03-7. The **"overdue" label** and any alert on a long-standing hold: E06-F03-6 registers
**XQ-06** because no document fixes a hold age, so this screen shows every open hold oldest first **with its age** and
calls none of them overdue; the `HoldOverdue` condition and its fifteen-minute evaluator are #33's. Turnaround,
workload and quality **analytics** — #45, which owns the `reporting` schema through #44. The workboard, the job queue
and the job card — #33 and #204. The delivery queue — #48. The TanStack Query cache and the role dashboards — #50,
which is **open**; this route must anticipate neither.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Branch Manager opens `/orders/exceptions`, moves between the five tabs by keyboard and by pointer, and each
      tab shows its view's rows **in the server's order** with its own filters and paging; the counts on screen equal
      what the API returned, and the screen asserts nothing the server did not send.
- [ ] From the failed-QC tab the garment's QC detail route (E06-F03-7's `/orders/:orderId/garment-jobs/:jobId/qc`) is
      reachable as a link, so the two client slices compose rather than duplicating the QC read.
- [ ] Each tab's "show more" fetches the next page with the server's cursor and appends without re-ordering or
      re-fetching the pages already shown; a `limit` is never sent above the server's maximum of 50.
- [ ] Accessibility: axe clean at the phone, tablet and desktop profiles, at 200% zoom and at 320 px;
      `expectNoHorizontalOverflow` green at its **default** five widths at both zoom levels, with the wide tables
      scrolling inside their own container rather than the page; each tab panel and its row count announced through a
      polite live region; tab controls at the 44 × 44 CSS px standard target (**AL-03**, proposed — see Open
      decisions); focus never obscured by a sticky bar.
- [ ] Localisation: no hard-coded English, both `ordersEn` and `ordersTa` complete (enforced by `pnpm typecheck`),
      the layout intact at the pseudo-locale's 40% growth and at the 150% product text size, and every date, age and
      amount rendered through the shared formatters with `DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE`.
- [ ] **Negative**: a caller without `orders.read` sees the `admin.forbidden.title`/`admin.forbidden.body`
      `EmptyState` and no table — asserted on those two message ids, not on a `Forbidden` component and not on a
      redirect; a `403` or `409` from the server renders as plain language through the problem alert, with the raw
      `failure` passed to the alert and **its message never rendered**.
- [ ] **Exception**: a tab whose view is empty renders its own empty state — "nothing is stuck here" — and not an
      error and not a `404`; a tab whose read **fails** renders `RetryableError` while the other four keep working,
      because the five reads are independent; and a tab whose read is slow renders `LoadingState` while the other
      four stay interactive.
