## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Branch Manager opens `/reports/stock/movements`, filters to one month and one category, reads the purchase and
      consumption quantities and values, and switches to the week bucket without losing the filter — against stubbed
      transport in the route test and over the real API in the evidence.
- [ ] A Branch Manager opens `/reports/stock/reorder` and reads which items are at or below their reorder point, with
      the posted variances beside them.
- [ ] Both screens show all five states: loading, empty (with the filter named), error (an `ApiProblem` through the
      shared alert, never the raw message), offline (`NetworkStatusBanner`, export action blocked rather than queued),
      and forbidden (`RequirePermission`'s `EmptyState` with `admin.forbidden.title` / `admin.forbidden.body` —
      **not** the `Forbidden` component, and **no** redirect).
- [ ] **Negative:** a caller without the cost gate sees the quantity columns, **no** money column, and the explanation
      line; the test asserts the money column **header is absent** rather than blank or zero.
- [ ] **Negative:** a `403` from a filter naming another branch is rendered as a refusal the reader can act on, and
      neither screen retries in a loop.
- [ ] **Exception:** a trend range above the synchronous limit shows the asynchronous offer from
      `reporting.range-too-large` through the shared export action, not an error; and a reorder list with **no** item
      at or below its point shows the empty state rather than an error, with the count announced as zero.
- [ ] `A11Y-DB-01`, `-02`, `-03`, `-07`, `-08` and `-09` pass on both screens, and **`A11Y-DB-04`, `-05` and `-06`**
      pass on the reorder screen: the shortfall is announced in words and units, the count of open items is announced
      on load, and the action from a row is a named control rather than an unlabelled chevron.
- [ ] `expectNoHorizontalOverflow` passes at 320, 360, 768, 1024 and 1280 px at 100% and 200%; `pnpm --dir clients/pwa
      lint`, `typecheck`, `test`, `build` and `format:check` are clean, and `generate:api:check` reports no drift.
