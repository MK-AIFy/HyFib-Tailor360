Sub-issue of #61. The second part of the plan's **#61c** user-acceptance work: the **money and oversight**
acceptance scripts — Cashier, Delivery Staff, Owner and Auditor — and `docs/uat/accountant-approval.md`, the
accountant's review of the billing examples that the parent's third acceptance criterion ("the accountant approves
billing examples") and `RE-15` both require. [E15-F03-8] created `docs/uat/` with its index, sign-off sheet and the
five shop-floor scripts; this one completes the set.

These four roles were separated from the five shop-floor roles because their scripts rest on different sources — the
dispatch gate, the cashier session, the cross-branch reach of Owner and Auditor, and the golden-master fixtures —
and because nine scripts plus an accountant document in one pull request is roughly 1,300 lines of
cross-referenced prose written from about 5,000 lines of source. Five and four.
