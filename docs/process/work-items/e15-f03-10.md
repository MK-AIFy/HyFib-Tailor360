  gate owners, section 7 what happens when a gate fails, and section 8 the relationship to the waiver register.
  **The pilot's severity rules are these; do not invent a second scale.**

  section 9 how the matrix is enforced. **This is the device list the pilot works through**; `OD-07` is its open

  (the ledger is the only authoritative source of stock, append-only with corrections), `INV-INV-01` (an invoice is a
  draft until posted; posting allocates the number under a row lock and freezes the row), `INV-CSH-03` (closing
  computes expected totals by mode, records counted totals and a denomination sheet, and requires a variance reason
  above the configured threshold), `INV-CDY-01` (scan events and custody transfers are append-only) and `INV-BID-01`
  (a barcode payload is globally unique and never re-issued). A reconciliation step cites the invariant it is
  checking.

  [E15-F03-6b]) — the automated half of the entry criteria.
- `docs/qa/dress-rehearsal.md` and `docs/qa/records/dress-rehearsal-001.md` (child [E15-F03-7]) — the rehearsal that
  must have passed before a pilot starts.
- `docs/uat/**` (child [E15-F03-8]) — the nine role scripts and `docs/uat/sign-off.md`, which the pilot window is
  when they are actually run.
- `docs/training/**` (child [E15-F03-9]) — the quick cards, the first-run card, the training label sheet and the
  support contacts the pilot hands to staff.
- `docs/prd/exceptions.md` and `docs/prd/walkthroughs.md` — the exception catalogue and the six journeys, so a pilot
  day's coverage can be measured against a list that already exists.
- The repository's **real labels**: `bug`, `enhancement`, `documentation`, `architecture`, `ci`, `chore` and
  `release-ready`. `release-ready` is applied and removed only by the `label` job of
  `.github/workflows/pr-policy.yml` and must never be set by hand; there is **no `security` label**, even though
  `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/security.yml` declares one. The triage document may only use the labels that exist.
- `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/` — the templates a pilot defect is raised with.

## Out of scope

- **Running the pilot.** A pilot is a fortnight of a real shop's work with real staff and real devices; no coding
  session can perform or sign it. This sub-issue ships the plan, the procedures and the empty record. The completed
  record is filed with the go/no-go by [E15-F03-11], which cannot be decided without it.
- The go/no-go decision, hypercare, the support rota and the release evidence index — [E15-F03-11].
- Training delivery and the user-acceptance run — [E15-F03-9] and [E15-F03-8] respectively; the pilot plan's entry
  criteria *reference* them.
- Writing or changing an operational runbook (#60), a monitoring dashboard or an alert (#58), or any deployment
  mechanism (#59).
- Changing a severity definition, a gate, a waiver rule or an invariant. The pilot applies them.
- Any application change. A defect the pilot finds is fixed by its own issue against its own module.
