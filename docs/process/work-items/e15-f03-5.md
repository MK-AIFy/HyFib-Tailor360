Sub-issue of #61. Part of the plan's **#61b**, and the first of the two exception-path children.
`docs/prd/exceptions.md` line 212 already assigns this work: every new exception "adds its row here, its transition
rows in `state-transitions.md` and its regression scenario to the business-scenario suite that **issue #61b
maintains**". That suite has never existed, and `docs/prd/walkthroughs.md` section 8 states plainly why the
walkthrough children cannot cover it: "no walkthrough ends in a cancellation, a refund, a credit note, a failed
delivery, a custody mismatch or a service-recovery case". This sub-issue automates the five exceptions that live in
intake, production and custody; the money, delivery and notification exceptions are [E15-F03-6]'s.

## What is already built (do not re-build)

- `tests/e2e/` — #52's Playwright configuration, browser and device projects, reporter list and page objects.
- `tests/e2e/scenarios/`, `support/dataset.ts`, `support/journey.ts`, `reporters/pass-matrix.ts` — child
  [E15-F03-3]. The reporter builds the matrix from spec tags, so this sub-issue adds specs and edits no shared file
  beyond appending rows to `tests/e2e/scenarios/README.md`.
- `tests/e2e/scenarios/w1-…` to `w6-…` — children [E15-F03-3] and [E15-F03-4]: the six happy-path walkthroughs.
  An exception scenario **starts from the state one of those journeys reaches** and diverges; reuse their steps
  through the journey fixture rather than re-scripting an intake.
- `docs/prd/exceptions.md` — the catalogue `EX-01` to `EX-15`, each with its trigger, the roles involved, the
  required handling and its own open decisions `XQ-01` to `XQ-05`. **This is the specification for every scenario
  below; the spec asserts what the row requires and nothing it does not.**
- `docs/prd/state-transitions.md` — the permitted transitions, including the exceptional ones. A scenario asserts a
  documented transition; it never defines one.
- `docs/nfr/support-matrix.md` section 7.1 — the scanning capability ladder: camera, hardware scanner, manual entry,
  and what each falls back to. `EX-07`'s manual fallback is specified there.
- `docs/prd/reviews/exception-review.md` — the record of which exception flows have been walked with staff and which
  have not (line 172 notes the cancelled-and-refunded walkthrough is proposed, not written).
- `tests/fixtures/manifest.json` — child [E15-F03-2]: fixed identifiers and codes.
