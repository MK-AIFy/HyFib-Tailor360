## What is already built (do not re-build)

- `src/Modules/Billing/Tailor360.Modules.Billing.Api/Invoicing/InvoiceEndpoints.cs` — three routes, with the exact
  preconditions each requires:
  - `CreateInvoiceDraft` — `POST /api/v1/billing/invoices`, body
    `{ "orderId": …, "calculationReference": "…", "garmentJobIds": [ … ] | null, "reason": … | null }`,
    `Idempotency-Key` required, **no `If-Match`**, answered `201` with `InvoicePayload` and a `Location` header.
  - `DiscardInvoiceDraft` — `POST /api/v1/billing/invoices/{invoiceId}/discard`, body `{ "reason": "…" }`
    (a reason is recorded: `.Audited(…, reasonRequired: true)`), `Idempotency-Key` **and** `If-Match` required.
  - `PostInvoice` — `POST /api/v1/billing/invoices/{invoiceId}/post`, body `{ "reason": … | null }`,
    `Idempotency-Key` **and** `If-Match` required; answered `200` with the posted `InvoicePayload` carrying
    `invoiceNumber`, `barcodePayload`, `financialYear`, `postedOn` and `postedAt`.
- `src/Modules/Billing/Tailor360.Modules.Billing.Domain/BillingErrors.cs` — the refusals this slice must explain in
  words, **grouped by the kind of problem the server answers, because a validation problem carries a field and a
  conflict does not**:
  - *Not found* (404): `billing.order-not-known`, `billing.calculation-not-found`, `billing.customer-not-found`,
    `billing.invoice-not-found`.
  - *Conflict* (409): `billing.order-not-yet-confirmed`, `billing.order-cancelled`,
    `billing.order-revised-since-draft`, `billing.job-already-invoiced`, `billing.snapshot-mismatch`,
    `billing.totals-mismatch`, `billing.calculation-for-another-branch`, `billing.branch-not-known`,
    `billing.invoice-not-editable`.
  - *Precondition failed* (409/412): `billing.invoice-changed` — "The invoice changed since it was read. Reload it
    and try again."
  - *Forbidden*: `billing.order-at-another-branch`.
  - *Validation, with a field* — render these **against the field** the problem names, not as a banner:
    `billing.value-required` (fields `branch`, `orderId`, `calculationReference` — note the code:
    `BillingErrors.Required(field)` yields **`billing.value-required`**, not `billing.required`),
    `billing.job-cancelled`, `billing.job-repeated`, `billing.line-not-a-garment-job`,
    `billing.configuration-missing`, `billing.reason-required`, `billing.reason-not-well-formed`. Each names the
    field it failed on; render it against that field, which is what `Problems.From` already puts in
    `errors` (RFC 9457, `CLAUDE.md` section 4 rule 3).
  **`billing.draft-number-conflict` is not an invoice refusal.** It exists on `BillingErrors` but is raised only by
  `PriceListStore` and `TaxConfigurationStore` (`grep -rn "BillingErrors.DraftNumberConflict" src/`), so do not map
  it here.
- `src/Modules/Billing/Tailor360.Modules.Billing.Api/Payloads/InvoiceRequests.cs` — `CreateInvoiceRequest` and the
  `BillingReasonRequest` shape used by discard and post.
- `docs/security/permission-matrix.md` section 5 rows 368 (`POST /invoices`, `billing.create_invoice`), 371
  (`POST /invoices/{id}/discard`, `billing.update_invoice`) and 372 (`POST /invoices/{id}/post`,
  `billing.post_invoice`) — all `current-branch`, all audited, all idempotent. Row 370 is the amend route this
  slice deliberately leaves alone. No matrix change is needed.
- `tests/Tailor360.IntegrationTests/Billing/InvoiceEndpointTests.cs` and `InvoicePostingTests.cs` — the server
  behaviour, including contiguous numbering under concurrent posts and a duplicate post replaying the original.
- `clients/pwa/src/routes/billing/InvoiceDetailRoute.tsx`, the `Invoice` types, `getInvoice` through
  `apiRequestVersioned` (so the `ETag` is already in hand) and the `billing.invoice.*` message family — all from
  **E09-F02-4**; the document view and the print controls from **E09-F02-5**.
- `clients/pwa/src/components/dialogs/ConfirmDialog.tsx` and `confirmTiers.ts` — the three tiers
  (`confirm`, `reason`, `typed`) and `resolveConfirmTier`, which turns `typed` into `reason` plus a second explicit
  press on a phone. `ConfirmDialogProps.irreversible` renders the fixed sentence "Cannot be undone — a supervisor
  correction is needed" as part of what is announced when the dialogue opens.
- `clients/pwa/src/routes/billing/DispatchExceptionApprovalRoute.tsx` and `AllocateAdvanceRoute.tsx` — the house

## Scope

- [ ] **Calls** in `clients/pwa/src/billing/billingApi.ts`, each through `apiRequestVersioned` so the fresh `ETag`
      comes back for the next write:
      `createInvoiceDraft({body, idempotencyKey})`, `discardInvoiceDraft({invoiceId, reason, version,
      idempotencyKey})`, `postInvoice({invoiceId, reason, version, idempotencyKey})`. The `version` is passed as
      `ifMatch`; types `CreateInvoiceDraftRequest` and `BillingReasonRequest` in
      `clients/pwa/src/billing/types.ts`, pinned against `clients/pwa/src/api/schema.d.ts`.
- [ ] **`InvoiceDraftRoute`** at `clients/pwa/src/routes/billing/InvoiceDraftRoute.tsx`, route
      `billing/invoices/new` under `RequirePermission permission={BILLING_PERMISSIONS.createInvoice}`. It reads
      `orderId` and `calculation` from the query string, posts the draft, and then shows the server's own lines and
      totals for review, with **Open this invoice** leading to `billing/invoices/:invoiceId`. **The screen never
      computes or proposes a figure**: the lines are the calculation's, and nothing is re-priced
      (matrix row 368). The optional `garmentJobIds` are offered only when the order fact names more jobs than the
      calculation priced, and the default is whatever the calculation priced — OD-22's default in force.
      Record in the component's own comment that the two parameters will be supplied by Orders' job-card screen
      (#204) once #201 merges, and that the route is reached by its parameters until then.
- [ ] **Post, on the detail screen**: a primary action on a `Draft` invoice, behind a `ConfirmDialog` of tier
      `typed` (so a phone gets `reason` plus a second press, A11Y-BI-13) with `irreversible` set. The dialogue
      announces the grand total and that a posted invoice cannot be edited (**A11Y-BI-10**). On success the screen
      announces the allocated invoice number in a polite live region and renders it as text that can be reached by
      keyboard (**A11Y-BI-11**), then refreshes the invoice so the new `ETag`, number, barcode payload and financial
      year are in hand.
- [ ] **Discard, on the detail screen**: a secondary action on a `Draft` invoice, behind a `ConfirmDialog` of tier
      `reason` with the reason mandatory, saying that the draft's garment jobs become free to invoice again. A
      posted invoice never offers it.
- [ ] **The `If-Match` discipline**: both writes send the `ETag` from the last read. A `412` or
      `billing.invoice-changed` is rendered as "somebody changed this invoice — here it is again", the invoice is
      refetched, and the action is **not** retried automatically.
- [ ] **The idempotency discipline**: the key is minted when the person presses the confirming control and held, on
      a fingerprint of the invoice and the action, until the call succeeds — so a retry, including the one
      `apiClient` performs after an in-place re-authentication, replays the first outcome rather than posting twice.
      Copy the `printKey` fingerprint pattern from `clients/pwa/src/billing/ReceiptPanel.tsx`.
- [ ] **Problem messages** for every code in the grouped list above — and **only** those codes — added to
      `clients/pwa/src/billing/billingProblems.ts`
      and `clients/pwa/src/i18n/messages/billing.ts` (both `billingEn` and `billingTa`, the Tamil entries carrying
      the English string with the file's existing `// not translated` comment). `billing.snapshot-mismatch` needs
      particular care: it means the stored calculation no longer reproduces on the versions it names, and the
      sentence must send the person back to re-price rather than suggest the invoice is wrong.
- [ ] **`OfflineBlockedAction`** around post, discard and draft creation: billing is online-only and none of these
      is ever queued (`clients/pwa/CLAUDE.md` section 6).
- [ ] **Router and navigation** wiring in `clients/pwa/src/app/router.tsx`, with `billing/invoices/new` guarded on
      `BILLING_PERMISSIONS.createInvoice`; the post and discard controls are additionally filtered by the
      permissions the session reports, and the server re-checks regardless.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Cashier holding `billing.create_invoice` opens `/billing/invoices/new?orderId=…&calculation=…`, the draft is
      created with an `Idempotency-Key`, and the screen shows the lines and the nine totals exactly as the server
      returned them — no figure on screen is computed by the client.
- [ ] On the detail screen, a `Draft` invoice offers **Post** and **Discard**; a `Posted` invoice offers neither.
- [ ] Posting shows a confirmation that announces the grand total and that the invoice cannot be edited afterwards;
      on success the allocated `invoiceNumber` is announced and rendered as reachable text, and the screen's state
      becomes `Posted`.
- [ ] Discarding requires a typed reason before the confirming control is enabled, and afterwards the invoice reads
      `Discarded`.
- [ ] **Negative:** a person holding `billing.create_invoice` but not `billing.post_invoice` sees no **Post**
      control, and a direct call is refused `403` and rendered as a sentence, never a code.
- [ ] **Negative:** pressing **Post** twice, or retrying after a failure, sends the **same** `Idempotency-Key` and
      yields one invoice with one number — asserted on the recorded request headers.
- [ ] **Negative:** a stale `ETag` (`412` / `billing.invoice-changed`) refetches the invoice, shows the
      "somebody changed this" sentence, and does **not** post; the reason the person typed is preserved.
- [ ] **Negative:** drafting against an order whose jobs are already invoiced renders
      `billing.job-already-invoiced` in plain language and names what to do instead, and drafting against a
      cancelled order renders `billing.order-cancelled`.
- [ ] **Exception:** `billing.snapshot-mismatch` renders a sentence that sends the person to re-price the order
      rather than implying the invoice or the price list is broken, and offers no control that would post anyway.
- [ ] **Exception:** offline, all three writes render `OfflineBlockedAction` ("Needs connection — this will not be
      queued") and issue no request; nothing is queued and nothing typed is lost when the connection returns.
- [ ] Every new state passes `expectNoAccessibilityViolations`, and the confirmations answer checklist records
      **A11Y-BI-10**, **A11Y-BI-11** and **A11Y-BI-13**.
