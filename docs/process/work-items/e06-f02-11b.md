Sub-issue of #33, and the second half of the Tailor Master's surface. E06-F02-11 builds the tablet workboard at
`/workboard`; this slice builds the **desktop planning board** at `/workboard/planning` — the same open phases grouped
by phase and then by assignee, with each assignee's workload beside their name. It exists as its own child on measured
evidence rather than on preference: `clients/pwa/src/routes/catalog/CatalogVersionEditorRoute.tsx` is 994 production
lines for one admin route and `clients/pwa/src/routes/catalog/CatalogDesignRoute.tsx` is 1,269, so a second non-trivial
route could not have shared E06-F02-11's pull request and stayed inside the 1,500-line target of CLAUDE.md section 6.

This is also the screen `docs/nfr/data-classification.md` section 5.15 and decision **DC-11** are really about. A
shared desktop browser left on a board of per-person open counts is a wall display, and DC-11 (line 721) says
throughput about a named member of staff is for the Tailor Master, the Branch Manager and the Owner and is never
published to peers. The route is therefore gated on `orders.assign`, not on `orders.read`.
