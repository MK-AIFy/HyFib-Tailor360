Sub-issue of #46. Fifth of thirteen: the **estimated** order and garment-job profitability projection — revenue minus
allocated material cost, minus configured labour and overhead, minus discounts and credits, minus wastage — with every
unavailable component recorded as unavailable and the figure labelled accordingly. It is split from its read endpoints
(`e10-f03-6`) because the computation, its completeness model and the contracts it reads are a full session on their
own, and because nothing of it exists: `grep -rn "profitab" src/` returns nothing.

**Read this before planning the work — what the published contracts can and cannot support.** This child must not
invent a linkage, and three are genuinely missing:

1. **Invoiced revenue is available at order level only, and the credit and debit notes do not name the order.**
   `src/Modules/Billing/Tailor360.Modules.Billing.Contracts/Events/InvoiceEvents.cs` publishes `InvoicePosted`
   (`billing.invoice-posted.v1`) with `EventId`, `OccurredAt`, `AggregateId`, `OrganisationId`, `BranchId`,
   `CustomerId`, `OrderId`, `InvoiceNumber`, `GrandTotal` and `Currency` — **no line breakdown, no discount total, no
   garment-job reference**. `InvoiceCancelled` carries `OrderId` and a `CreditDocumentId`. **`CreditNotePosted` and
   `DebitNotePosted` carry `InvoiceId` and no `OrderId` at all** — so this child must keep the invoice → order mapping
   it learns from `InvoicePosted` and resolve the notes through it; a note whose invoice has not been seen is held,
   not dropped, and not attributed to a guess. Note also that every amount on these events is a **`decimal` plus a
   `Currency` string**, not a `Money`: rehydrate `new Money(grandTotal, currency)` before any arithmetic, and never
   add two currencies (the `Money` operators refuse it).
2. **Quoted revenue is available per garment job**, through
   `Orders.Contracts.Orders.IOrderSnapshotQuery.GetPricedAsync(orderId, callerPermissions, ct)`
   (`src/Modules/Orders/Tailor360.Modules.Orders.Contracts/Orders/IOrderSnapshotQuery.cs` line 160), which answers
   `PricedOrderSnapshot(Order, TotalsIncluded, Totals, JobTotals)`. `Totals` and each `GarmentJobPricedTotals.Totals`
   are a `PricedTotals` carrying `CatalogVersionId`, `PriceListVersionId`, `TaxConfigurationVersionId`, then
   `Subtotal`, `DiscountTotal`, `TaxableValue`, `CentralTax`, `StateTax`, `IntegratedTax`, `Cess`, `RoundOff`,
   `GrandTotal` (all `Money`) and `CalculatedAt`. `JobTotals` is **keyed** by `GarmentJobId`, not positional. Two
   things its own doc comments say, and a test written against the opposite will fail: `callerPermissions` is taken
   but **"none is read today"**, and `TotalsIncluded` is **"true on every answer today"** — the mask is a forward
   declaration waiting on OD-13. So **do not write a test that expects a masked answer**; do write the handler so that
   `TotalsIncluded == false` records `job_level_revenue_not_published` rather than a zero, because that is the
   behaviour the mask will produce when it lands. The read is **per order**, so the recompute pass batches by order
   and must not call it once per garment job.
3. **Per-movement material cost is not published.** `inventory.stock-consumed.v1` carries the ledger entry, item,
   location, base quantity, job and phase — and **no unit cost** (Inventory keeps cost behind
   `inventory.view_valuation`). So allocated material cost is `consumed base quantity × the unit value IValuationQuery
   reports for that item and location at the consumption date's cut-off`, stored with the valuation run that answered
   and flagged as a valuation-derived basis rather than an actual purchase cost. #184 records the same class of gap
   for the pricing snapshot reference and the instruction is the same: flag it, do not guess around it. If a reviewer
   wants actual cost per movement, that is an **additive** Inventory event or read contract raised against #39 — never
   a cross-schema read.

## What is already built (do not re-build)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For a confirmed multi-garment order with a posted invoice, recorded consumption, one wastage movement and a
      published assumption version, every component is populated, `is_complete` is true, and the margin equals the
      components under the documented formula — asserted against a fixture under `tests/fixtures/reporting/`, not
      against a number recomputed by the test.
- [ ] With **no** published costing assumption version, the labour and overhead components are recorded unavailable
      with reason `no_published_assumption_version`, the margin is marked estimated and incomplete, and **no row is
      silently computed as if labour were zero**.
- [ ] Replaying every event twice produces one row per grain; `rebuild-projection` reproduces every row
      byte-identically.
- [ ] Publishing a **new** assumption version leaves every existing row unchanged and adds the recomputed row naming
      the new version; a historical figure is still answerable at its own version.
- [ ] **Negative:** an invoice that is later cancelled removes its contribution through the `InvoiceCancelled` handler
      and the compensating credit note, and never by editing the original contribution away — the cancellation's
      effect is visible as its own component, and the projection is not hand-corrected.
- [ ] **Negative:** a cut-off with no completed valuation run records material cost unavailable with reason
      `no_valuation_run_at_cutoff`; nothing is valued at zero.
- [ ] **Exception:** a cancelled garment job keeps its projected row with its costs and no revenue, flagged so that a
      margin report can exclude it per the metric dictionary rather than losing the history.
- [ ] ARCH-005 and ARCH-011 hold: no `billing.*`, `orders.*` or `inventory.*` table is read, and no Reporting project
      references another module's `Infrastructure` or `Api`.
