Sub-issue of #56. The W5 half of this parent owes an "automated security regression suite" over eleven abuse-case
families (plan Section 8), split across seven sibling sub-issues. **This sub-issue takes one family: `ABF-07` malicious
upload, with the authorised-streaming half that is inseparable from it.** It is one family rather than four because a
hostile-upload suite needs a **hostile corpus** — a polyglot, an archive, an SVG carrying script, a type that disagrees
with its bytes, a file over the cap — and building, justifying and classifying that corpus is the bulk of the work.
`ABF-09` server-side request forgery is its own sibling, because it is the one family in the catalogue with no
implementation to test and needs a decision rather than a corpus. `ABF-10` credential abuse and `ABF-11` denial of
service are a third sibling, because both already have substantial machinery to extend and neither needs a corpus at
all.

This is the family that answers the parent's **fourth** acceptance criterion — "malicious upload and unauthorized
media/export tests pass" — for uploads and media. The export half belongs to the `ABF-08` sibling.

**The whole subject of this sub-issue is unbuilt today**, and that is the first thing to re-check at W5 entry:
`find src/Modules/Media -name "*.cs" | wc -l` returns **7**, all scaffolding. `Media.Api/MediaEndpoints.cs` declares
the group prefix `/api/v1/media` and the `Media` OpenAPI tag and maps an **empty** group — zero endpoints.
`Media.Infrastructure/MediaModuleServiceCollectionExtensions.cs:14` declares `SchemaName = "media"`, and there is no
`DbContext`, no migration and therefore no `media` schema in the database. The pipeline is **#187** (schema, upload
pipeline, quarantine, malware scan) and **#188** (authorised streaming and the access log), with **#192** for
retention and controlled deletion. So this suite is the first thing that will hold that pipeline to its rules
(`NFR-SE-11`, `NFR-PR-03`), and if neither #187 nor #188 has shipped by the time this is picked up, see the first
bullet of **Scope**.
