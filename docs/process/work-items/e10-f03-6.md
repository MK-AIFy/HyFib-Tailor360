Sub-issue of #46. Sixth of thirteen: the HTTP surface over the profitability projection of `e10-f03-5`, including the
**"method and assumptions" payload** the parent issue's second acceptance criterion requires — "profitability reports
display calculation method, assumptions and unavailable components" — and the margin column gate. It is split from the
projection because the projection is a full session, and from `e10-f03-3` because the margin columns are a different
classification problem from the stock columns and because both children edit `docs/security/field-visibility.md` and
must not do so at the same time.

## What is already built (do not re-build)

- `e10-f03-5` — `projections_job_profitability` with every component, its availability flag and reason code, the
  stored estimated-and-incomplete label, and the provenance identifiers. **This child computes nothing**: a handler
  reads projected rows and shapes a payload.
- `e10-f03-4` — `costing_assumption_versions` and its read routes. The assumptions panel names the version a row was
  computed with and links to it; it does not copy the rates into a second place.
- `e10-f03-3` — `ReportingResponseViews.cs`, the new `ViewSurface.Management` member, the line in
  `ApplicationResponseViews.All` that composes it into `ResponseViewCatalogue`, and the
  `docs/security/field-visibility.md` sections that declare them. **Extend them; do not introduce a second surface and
  do not add a second `IResponseViewSource`.**
- #186 — `GET /api/v1/reporting/status?report=…` for the status strip. Reuse it.
- `src/Modules/Billing/Tailor360.Modules.Billing.Api/Payments/ReconciliationEndpoints.cs` — the endpoint idiom.
- `docs/reports/metrics.md` — the profitability entries `e10-f03-5` wrote. The method panel's text is assembled from
  `metric_definitions` rows, not from strings hard-coded in a payload.

## What is missing

- No route answers a profitability figure; `docs/api/openapi.v1.json` has no Reporting operation at all beyond what
  the earlier children added.
- No payload expresses "this component is unavailable and here is why" — the availability flags exist in the
  projection from `e10-f03-5` and have never been exposed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An Owner reads a garment job's profitability over HTTP and receives every component, the margin, the method
      and-assumptions block naming the costing assumption version and the valuation run, and the data-as-of stamp —
      with no direct database access in the test.
- [ ] A row computed with **no published assumption version** is returned with labour and overhead marked unavailable,
      their reason code, and the estimated-and-incomplete label — and the test asserts the label is the stored one
      rather than one the endpoint decided.
- [ ] Every route declares exactly one permission and one rate-limit policy, changes nothing so carries no audit
      filter, satisfies ARCH-023 explicitly, and has exactly one row in permission-matrix section 5.
- [ ] **Negative:** a caller holding `reports.read` without the margin gate receives the completeness and identifying
      columns with **every money column withheld** — absent, not zero, not an error.
- [ ] **Negative:** a Branch Manager assigned to branch A requesting a branch-B job's row by editing `{jobId}` is
      answered **`404 security.resource-not-found`**, **identically** to an identifier matching no row at all, and a
      branch-B filter on the list route is refused `403 security.forbidden`; no aggregate anywhere discloses a
      branch-B figure.
- [ ] **Exception:** a cancelled garment job's row is returned with its costs, no revenue and its exclusion flag, per
      the metric dictionary — it is neither hidden nor silently counted in a margin total.
- [ ] `docs/security/field-visibility.md` and the code agree under every row of its section 8 assertion table —
      thirteen rows, numbered 1 to 12 with an extra **8b**.
