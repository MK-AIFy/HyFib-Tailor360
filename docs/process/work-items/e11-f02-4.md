## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **The journey:** a dispatched garment is recorded as failed with a configured reason; the authorisation is spent,
      a pending custody transfer back to the branch exists, the queue entry is `queued` again with `reopened_count = 1`,
      and exactly one `custody.delivery-failed.v1` is published — over HTTP, with no direct database access.
- [ ] A second receive scan for the same order is evaluated from scratch and refuses if the balance has grown since the
      first authorisation, which is what makes the "re-evaluated from scratch" note of the matrix true.
- [ ] Both routes declare a permission with branch scope, a resource scope, exactly one rate-limit policy, the audit
      filter with `reasonRequired: true` and idempotency (ARCH-007, ARCH-008, ARCH-017, ARCH-023).
- [ ] **Negative:** an outcome with no reason code, or with a code not in the configured list, is refused with
      field-level problem details and records nothing. A reason is never defaulted.
- [ ] **Negative:** an outcome against an authorisation that was never dispatched, or that is already spent, is refused;
      the spent authorisation can never be re-used to dispatch, and a test asserts the refusal rather than a silent
      second dispatch.
- [ ] **Negative:** a caller without `custody.record_delivery_outcome`, or from another branch, is refused and audited.
- [ ] **Exception:** a dispute leaves the `custody.delivery_confirmations` row byte-for-byte unchanged apart from its
      new `dispute_case_id`, opens exactly one case for repeated submissions of the same client event UUID, and the
      timeline afterwards shows the delivery **and** the dispute side by side rather than one replacing the other.
- [ ] **Exception:** a replayed outcome with its original `Idempotency-Key` returns the first outcome, creates no second
      transfer and does not increment `reopened_count` twice.
- [ ] The customer timeline for the order's customer shows the dispatch, the delivery or its failure and the dispute,
      newest first, with no recipient name, no signature, no amount and no free text; a caller holding no Custody read
      permission sees none of the Custody entries and still receives the rest of the timeline (the composer's
      partial-source contract).
