## Out of scope

  `clients/pwa/src/auth/types.ts` line 107 and `UserPreferences.cs` line 106 both carry `landingRoute` — and
  **nothing in the client honours it**: `grep -rn "landingRoute" clients/pwa/src` returns only the type, the two
  fixtures and the generated schema. Teaching the shell to honour a landing preference is a change to
  `clients/pwa/src/app/router.tsx`'s index route that affects every role, and it belongs with the shell rather than
  with a report screen. Recorded here as the follow-up it is; the screen is reachable from the existing **Reports**
  destination and from the hub on the day it merges.
- **The low-stock tile.** Its figure is Inventory's, and the stock area is **Confidential** rather than Internal —
  `docs/nfr/data-classification.md` section 5.9. The reads belong to #40's operational screens and #46's inventory

  payments,
  receivables and export screens (#184, #185, #186). Any export. **The operational UAT script and its run** — both are

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An Owner follows the existing **Reports** destination to E10-F02-7a's hub, opens Today on a phone-width
      viewport, reads every tile with its label and its period, hears the number of tiles needing attention, and
      reaches each tile's register by its drill-through (**A11Y-RJ-08** steps 1, 2 and 7). A **Branch Manager** holding
      `reports.read` reaches the same screen by URL or from the hub, because `ROLE_NAVIGATION` has no
      `branch-manager` key — a gap E10-F02-7a recorded and this slice does not fix.
- [ ] **Negative — a tile this role may not read:** a Branch Manager without the permission behind a tile sees the
      permission-denied tile state naming the permission and who to ask — not a zero, not a dash and not an error
      (A11Y-RJ-08 step 6).
- [ ] **Negative — forbidden route:** a Tailor reaching `/reports/today` sees the `RequirePermission` empty state
      with `admin.forbidden.title` / `admin.forbidden.body`, and is not redirected.
- [ ] **Negative — a failed read:** one tile's read failing leaves the other tiles readable and shows that tile's
      retry; a single `500` does not blank the screen.
- [ ] **Negative — offline:** the shell banner says so, nothing is queued, and no figure is presented as current.
- [ ] **Exception — a deferred tile:** with #184's and #185's reads absent from
      `clients/pwa/src/api/schema.d.ts`, the screen renders the tiles it can and the pull request lists the rest as
      deferred; a test asserts the absent tile is not rendered at all.
- [ ] **Exception — the screen holds still:** no timer re-orders or replaces a figure under the reader's hands; a
      test asserts no interval is started.
- [ ] No axe violation and no horizontal page scroll at any of the five default widths at 100% and 200% zoom.
