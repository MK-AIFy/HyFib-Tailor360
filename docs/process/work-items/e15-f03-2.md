  set: branch `CBE01`, financial year `2627`, `O-CBE01-2627-000512`, `J-…-01`, `E-CBE01-2627-000418`,
  `C-CBE01-004182`, barcode `G-` plus eleven Crockford base32 characters plus a check character, namespaces `S-`,

  same. Two warnings that section 1.2 states about itself and that the manifest must respect: `CBE01` is

  character but is not computed with the production checksum"**. A fixture payload is therefore generated through the

  check character. The catalogue states this in one line, because it is the mistake the shared shapes invite.
- `docs/prd/category-hierarchy.md` (category, service-type and template codes) and `docs/prd/measurement-templates.md`
  (the proposed field sets) — where the catalogue codes come from, under **OD-10**.
- `.github/coverage-floors.json` — the measured per-project coverage floors the `build-test-dotnet` job enforces.
  Its `exclude_projects` list is `["*Tests"]`, matched with `fnmatch` by `scripts/coverage-floor.py` line 137, and

  the default floor unless it is excluded — see Scope.
- `Directory.Build.props` — repository-wide build settings every project inherits: `net10.0`, `Nullable=enable`,

## Data and classification

Per `docs/nfr/data-classification.md` section 2. Every value in every fixture is synthetic; that is the whole
point of the library, and it is what `CLAUDE.md` section 4 rule 10 and `NFR-MQ-04` require.

| Item | Class | Purpose | Retention | Who may read it | Logs, telemetry, export |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tests/fixtures/**` dataset files | Public (synthetic) | Make a journey, a rule or a calculation reproducible | As the repository | Anyone with repository access | Freely quotable in a pull request. A value that resembles a real customer, telephone number or measurement must be changed, not redacted |
| `manifest.json` `fixedIdentifiers` | Public | Let a test, a walkthrough and a training script name the same branch and order | As the repository | Anyone with repository access | Safe in output. They are UUIDv7-shaped constants, not production identifiers |
| The golden masters under `tests/fixtures/billing/` | Public (synthetic), **evidentiary** | Prove a pricing or tax calculation reproduces what the accountant approved | Kept for the life of the repository; never edited in place | Anyone with repository access; the accountant reviews them under OD-05 | Quoted in the accountant approval of child [E15-F03-8b]. Contains amounts, no person |
| The new project's source | Internal | Load the manifest and open a dataset | As the repository | Anyone with repository access | No secret, no connection string, no environment variable is read by the loader |

Nothing personal, financial-in-the-real-sense, or credential-bearing is introduced. The catalogue carries one line
saying that a fixture may never be produced by copying, masking or subsetting production data (`CLAUDE.md` section
4 rule 10), because masking is the mistake this rule exists to forbid.
