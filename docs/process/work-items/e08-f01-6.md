      and two locations, publishes it from draft to active, then retires it and reactivates it — entirely through
      the screen, proved by a Vitest journey against the stubbed transport and recorded as evidence.

      reactivation rather than edit controls that would be refused (#38 acceptance criterion 4, client half; the
      purchase and issue refusals themselves are #195's status guard and #39's ledger check).

      selection; a retired location is not offered in the availability list.
- [ ] Every quantity and factor on both screens carries its unit, in text, and is formatted through
      `getFormatters().formatQuantity` — no bare number, no inline formatting call.
- [ ] A duplicate SKU or barcode is shown as a field error on the field that caused it, from the server's RFC 9457
      problem through `plainLanguageDetail`, with the typed input intact — never a raw code and never a toast.
- [ ] **Concurrency, verified against the stub.** When a stubbed read carries an `ETag`, the save sends the same
      value as `If-Match` (asserted from `RecordedCall`); a stubbed `409` carrying `currentVersion` shows the
      current value beside what was typed, offers the choice, and a retry reuses the **same** `Idempotency-Key`

      `If-Match` and never produces a `428`.

      whose every control ends in a 403. A caller holding `manage_items` but **not** `manage_suppliers` or

      carries rather than breaking.
- [ ] The barcode is rendered exactly as received and never assembled, prefixed or check-charactered in the client.
- [ ] Offline, a write is refused in place with "this will not be queued"; no item edit is ever queued.
- [ ] No user-facing literal anywhere in the new components, and `pnpm --dir clients/pwa typecheck` proves `ta-IN`
      carries every key.
