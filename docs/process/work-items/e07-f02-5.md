Sub-issue of #36. Follows E07-F02-3 and E07-F02-3b. **This is the bottom of the ladder**: the authorised manual path a
person takes when the camera is refused, absent or busy, or when the label is damaged — and the recovery that stops a
permission denial from dead-ending the workflow, which is the parent's second acceptance criterion in its own words.
Without it the scan screen has exactly two rungs and `docs/nfr/support-matrix.md` section 6 promises three: manual
entry is the only row in that matrix marked "**Yes — always**" for every one of the eight device classes.

The gap is checkable: `clients/pwa/src/scanning/` after E07-F02-3 contains the camera and wedge sources and no
`ManualEntrySource`; `clients/pwa/src/auth/apiClient.ts`'s `ApiRequestOptions` (lines 137-178) declares `method`,
`body`, `signal`, `challengeOnUnauthenticated`, `challengeOnStepUp`, `idempotencyKey` and `ifMatch` and **no way to
send any other header**, so the `X-Scan-Source: manual` that #194's endpoints require cannot be sent today at all.
