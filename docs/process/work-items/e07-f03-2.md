Sub-issue of #37. E07-F03-1 records a scan against whoever already holds the garment; it cannot move one. The
two-sided hand-off — transfer out, then receive by the destination — is the transition `docs/prd/state-transitions.md`
section 4.1 gives four rows to and `docs/architecture/invariants.md` INV-CDY-04 makes an invariant, and nothing of it
exists: `grep -rn "custody_transfers" src/` returns nothing and

with `custody.reject_transfer` and "Rejection of a transfer" in its permission and exception columns rather than a
third route spelled out — are not there. The third route is this slice's reading of that row and is named as such, not
quoted. This slice builds the transfer inside one branch; the cross-branch grant and the overdue sweep are E07-F03-4.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A **Branch Manager** holding `custody.transfer_out` transfers two jobs to a named **Tailor Master** over
      HTTP; that Tailor Master, holding `custody.receive`, accepts, and the response and `job_custody_state` both
      name them as custodian, with the accepting user, the time and the resulting custody recorded on the transfer
      and on both scan events (#37's first acceptance criterion). **The receiver is deliberately not a Tailor.**
      `docs/prd/state-transitions.md` section 4.1 line 286 says "Tailor Master, Tailor, QC or Reception
      (`custody.receive`)", but the approved matrix line 175 grants `custody.receive` to
      `branch_manager`, `tailor_master`, `delivery_staff` only, and `SystemRoles.cs:330-331` gives the Tailor role
      exactly `custody.scan` and `custody.manual_lookup`. **Do not add a grant to close the gap** — record the
      disagreement between line 286 and matrix line 175 in the pull request as a finding for **OD-13**, which is
      where role grants are settled, and build against the grants as seeded (`CLAUDE.md` section 8: a number or a
      grant with no source is a product decision).
- [ ] Every route added declares one permission, one rate-limit policy, the audit filter, and either a resource
      scope or a recorded justification (ARCH-007, ARCH-008, ARCH-017, ARCH-023).
- [ ] **Negative — second pending transfer.** Transferring a job that already has a `pending` transfer is refused
      `custody.transfer-already-pending` by the partial unique index as well as by the handler.
- [ ] **Negative — not the destination.** A user who is neither the named destination nor a member of the
      destination team is refused `custody.not-the-destination` on receive, writes no scan event, and the transfer
      stays `pending`.
- [ ] **Negative — other branch, and it is a 404.** A caller who **holds** the permission in another branch and
      names a transfer whose `from_branch_id` is not theirs gets `404 security.resource-not-found` on receive,
      reject and read — `matrix.yaml`'s `holder-other-branch: out-of-reach` dimension, the same answer as a
      transfer that does not exist, so `{transferId}` cannot be used to enumerate transfers. A caller who does not
      hold the permission at all gets `403 security.forbidden` (`non-holder-own-branch`). Both are audited as
      `authz.denied`. The cross-branch **grant** is E07-F03-4 and must fail here.
- [ ] **Negative — replay.** Receiving twice with the same `Idempotency-Key` replays the first outcome; receiving
      an already `accepted` transfer with a fresh key is `409 custody.transfer-not-pending`.
- [ ] **Exception — rejection.** A rejection without a reason is refused; with one, the transfer is `rejected`,
      the garment is **still the sender's**, and the reason is on the audit row.
- [ ] The database refuses every `UPDATE` to a resolved transfer and every `DELETE`, proved against PostgreSQL.
