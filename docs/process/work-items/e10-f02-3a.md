## Scope

- [ ] **`reporting.projections_phase_assignment`** — append-only projection rows, one per assignment act:
      `organisation_id`, `branch_id`, `garment_job_id`, `phase_code`, `category_key`, `workflow_version_id`,
      `assignee_user_id`, `assigned_at`, `superseded_at`, `unassigned_at`, `last_event_at`. A reassignment writes a
      new row and stamps the previous one; nothing is ever updated in place except that stamp, so an earlier
      completion stays attributed to the person who held the phase when it happened.
- [ ] **`reporting.projections_assignee_workload_daily`** — `organisation_id`, `assignee_user_id`, `branch_id`,
      `business_date` (the branch's own day), `phases_completed`, `phases_completed_on_time`, `phases_completed_late`,
      `phases_assigned`, `phases_reassigned_away`, `last_event_at`. The daily grain is what makes a throughput
      figure reproducible on rebuild; the **current** open-work figure is computed at read time by E10-F02-3b and is
      deliberately not stored.
- [ ] **Handlers** `reporting.workload-on-job-assigned`, `-on-job-reassigned`, `-on-job-unassigned` and
      `-on-job-phase-changed` (the completion attribution), following `OrderFactHandlers.cs`: one handler per event
      type, unique `HandlerName`, `Schema = ReportingDbContext.SchemaName`, writes staged and never saved, safe to
      call twice.
- [ ] **Out-of-order and duplicate delivery are the normal case.** A phase completion may arrive before the
      assignment that explains it, in which case the completion is attributed when the assignment row lands and the
      daily row is corrected by recomputation rather than by an increment; the inbox row, not a guard in the
      projector, is what makes a redelivery a no-op.
- [ ] **No display name, anywhere.** The projection stores `assignee_user_id` only. 5.15 allows the identifier and
      forbids the name in a log, a metric or a trace attribute, and a name stored in a projection is a name that
      goes stale the day somebody marries.
- [ ] **One migration** on the Reporting chain, named `AssigneeWorkloadProjection`, scaffolded with the command in
      `src/Modules/CLAUDE.md` section 6 against `ReportingDbContext`, producing `<timestamp>_AssigneeWorkloadProjection`
      as the Billing chain is named (`20260912151015_Payments.cs`). Expand-only: two new tables, nothing altered.
- [ ] **Registration** of the handlers and the projection in
      `src/Modules/Reporting/Tailor360.Modules.Reporting.Infrastructure/ReportingModuleServiceCollectionExtensions.cs`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Over a synthetic stream published through `IOrdersEventPublisher` and dispatched — assign a phase to tailor A,
      complete it, assign the next phase to A, reassign it to B, complete it — `projections_phase_assignment` holds
      three rows with the first two stamped `superseded_at` or closed, and `projections_assignee_workload_daily`
      credits the first completion to A and the second to B.
- [ ] **Reassignment does not rewrite history:** the earlier completion stays attributed to A after the
      reassignment, asserted directly on the daily rows and not inferred from a total.
- [ ] **Negative — redelivery:** dispatching the same stream twice changes no row and leaves the checkpoint unmoved
      on the second pass.
- [ ] **Negative — out of order:** a completion delivered before its assignment ends with the same daily rows as
      the forward order, with no row double-counted and no completion left unattributed once the assignment lands.
- [ ] **Exception — an unassigned phase:** a phase completed while nobody held it produces no assignee row and is
      counted only against the unassigned figure, so the unassigned population is never silently absorbed into
      somebody's throughput.
- [ ] **Exception — a member of staff who has left:** a completion by somebody later deactivated stays on the daily
      row under their identifier; the projection holds no active flag and no name, and resolving either is
      E10-F02-3b's read-time concern.
- [ ] **Rebuild equals live** (#45's fifth acceptance criterion): `rebuild-projection` over the same stream
      reproduces both tables row for row, including the daily attribution.
- [ ] `ReportingDbContext` maps nothing outside `reporting` (ARCH-005); no Reporting project references
      `Orders.Infrastructure`, `Orders.Api`, `Identity.Infrastructure` or `Identity.Api` (ARCH-011).
