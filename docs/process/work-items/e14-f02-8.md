| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| The `requestId` in the path | Internal | Address the request | As the browser's history | The reader | A request identifier is a UUIDv7 and names no person, so it is safe in a path — unlike a **subject** identifier, which stays out of the URL |
