## Scope

- [ ] **`clients/pwa/src/inventory/unitConversion.ts`** (+ `unitConversion.test.ts`) — a plain module, not logic
      inside a component. Given an item's base, purchase and issue units and their factors it answers:
      - **(a) Does each factor round-trip to full precision?** The guarantee, documented at the top of the module
        the way `units.ts` documents its own: converting a quantity out of the base unit and back returns the same
        value exactly, at the precision `formatQuantity` renders (four decimal places at most). A factor that does
        not is refused with the two units named.
      - **(b) Is the unit graph acyclic?** Across base, purchase and issue and any further unit #195's payload
        carries, detect a cycle and name the two units that close it.
      - **(c) The conversion in words**, for the visible text and for the accessible name: "1 roll = 25 metres;
        2 rolls = 50 metres". One function, so the sentence is identical in both places.
      - **(d) Which field a failure belongs to**, so the message lands on the factor the person must change rather
        than on the form.
      It is a **courtesy, not an authority**: #195 validates and its refusal is what the person sees when the two
      disagree. The module says so in its own header comment.
- [ ] **Unit and conversion types appended to `clients/pwa/src/inventory/types.ts`** — `ItemUnit`,
