Sub-issue of #49. **Evidence only. No production code, no migration, no endpoint, no screen.** This issue exists so
that no coding session in this parent is blocked on a physical device, a human walking a journey, or a browser tool a
headless agent cannot drive. Its deliverable is recorded evidence in `docs/` and in the pull request, nothing more.

**Why it is a separate issue.** `docs/process/definition-of-done.md` DoD 7 verifies the automated accessibility check
through "axe in the Playwright run" and the journey's network-failure step through "the Playwright run", and
**neither `tests/e2e` nor `playwright.config` exists in this repository** — `scripts/dev` says the Playwright suite
arrives with **#52**. `docs/nfr/support-matrix.md` line 327 makes recorded real-device evidence a **release** gate
("Every release; stored with the release evidence index"), in a browser tab and in installed mode, per Tier 1 device
class. And the parent's own required verification asks for "**UAT** for positive, negative and alteration-request
journeys", which is a person at a device, not a test run. Attaching any of that to a coding pull request would either
block it indefinitely or invite a reviewer to accept a claim in place of an artefact, which `CLAUDE.md` section 7 is
explicit about: "A pull request is done when a second person can see that it is done" and "'Tested locally' is not
evidence."

**The Definition of Ready already anticipates this split.** `docs/process/definition-of-ready.md` DOR-16 requires that
"the session can actually build and test what the issue needs — the .NET software development kit for backend work, a
database and object store for integration tests, **a device or printer for a physical rehearsal**", and its own
optional-criteria table (line 86) lists "DOR-16 physical hardware" as applying to "#35, #36, #37, #48 and #61c
always; others rarely". **#49 is not in that list.** Separating the rehearsal from the coding slices is therefore how
#49's children satisfy DOR-16 — each coding child declares a dev container and a database, and this child declares
the devices and the people.

## What this issue is NOT

- Not a waiver. `docs/process/waivers.md` is for a **gate that cannot be met**, not for evidence that a human has not
  yet produced. Nothing here is waived; it is scheduled.
- Not a test-automation issue. The Playwright suite, its axe integration and the network-failure step are **#52**'s.
  If #52 has merged by the time this runs, automate what it can carry and record only what remains.
- Not an excuse to thin the coding siblings' own verification. Every machine-checkable assertion stays where it is:
  `expectNoHorizontalOverflow` at its five default widths and 200% zoom, the jsdom axe run, the pseudo-locale story,
  the state stories and the Vitest journeys are all in [E11-F03-2], [E11-F03-6] and [E11-F03-6b] and are **not**
  deferred here.
- Not #61's release evidence index. This issue **feeds** it; it does not replace it.

## Scope

- [ ] **UAT walk, three journeys**, with a member of staff or a product reviewer driving and a coding contributor
      observing, against a seeded synthetic dataset (`dotnet run --project src/Tools/Tailor360.Cli -- seed-synthetic`,
      noting that it currently covers only Identity and Customers, so the order, delivery and feedback fixtures are
      built by hand in the walk):
      **positive** — a delivered order, an invitation, five high ratings, no case opened;
      **negative** — a rating at or below the branch threshold, exactly one case opened, assigned, contacted, closed
      with a resolution, closure confirmation suppressed or sent according to the customer's consent;
      **alteration request** — a high rating with an explicit alteration request, one case opened, the alteration
      accepted on close, and the hand-off either recorded against Orders (if #34 has merged) or refused with
      `notifications.alteration-hand-off-unavailable` and the case left open (if it has not). **Record which.**
- [ ] **Real-device installed-mode walk** of `/service-recovery` and `/service-recovery/:caseId` on one Tier 1 Android
      phone and one Tier 1 iPhone or iPad, in a browser tab **and** in installed mode, with the device, operating
      system and browser versions logged — the shape `docs/nfr/support-matrix.md` line 327 specifies. Record the
      result in the installed-mode column of that document.
- [ ] **Real-device browser walk of the customer page** `/c/feedback/{token}` on the same two devices, in a browser
      tab only: the page is served outside the PWA shell and outside the service-worker scope, so installed mode does
      not apply to it and that is worth stating rather than leaving as a gap.
- [ ] **Browser axe run** over the two client routes, each of the three case dialogs, and the server-rendered
      feedback page in both locales, with no serious or critical violation. A browser extension or a one-off script
      against the running host is acceptable evidence while #52 is open; attach the report.
- [ ] **Keyboard-only walk** of both client routes including each dialog's focus trap and focus restoration, and of
      the customer page's form, error summary and submit. Record the tab order actually observed.
- [ ] **200% zoom and 320 px read** of all three surfaces, by eye, in English and Tamil — the thing
      `expectNoHorizontalOverflow` cannot tell you, which is whether the page is still *usable* rather than merely
      free of horizontal scroll.
- [ ] **Completed `docs/nfr/a11y-checklist.md`** for the three new journeys — the queue, the case, and the customer
      page — attached as the artefact DoD 7 asks for ("The completed checklist attached as evidence. Human — author,
      spot-checked by the reviewer").
- [ ] **The Tamil read**, by somebody who reads Tamil, of every new string in
      `clients/pwa/src/i18n/messages/feedback.ts` and of the customer page's server-side Tamil set. A pseudo-locale
      story proves the layout survives growth; it proves nothing about whether the words are right.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All three UAT journeys are walked end to end and recorded, each with the date, the participants, the build
      (commit hash), the device and the outcome. A journey that could not be completed is recorded as **not
      completed, with the reason and the blocking issue**, never as a pass.
- [ ] The installed-mode rows of `docs/nfr/support-matrix.md` carry the two device walks with their versions.
- [ ] The completed `docs/nfr/a11y-checklist.md` for the three journeys is attached, and every item is answered —
      including the ones answered "not applicable", each with its one-line reason.
- [ ] The axe reports show no serious or critical violation on any of the three surfaces in either locale. A
      violation found is filed against its owning sibling and linked; it is **not** fixed here.
- [ ] Negative: the walk deliberately exercises the refusals, and each behaves as its sibling's body says —
      an expired link, a second edit after the window, a rating of 0, an alteration ticked with no garment, a command
      with no reason, a command while offline, a case of another branch, and a close with an accepted alteration while
      the Orders hand-off is unregistered. **A walk that only exercises the happy path does not satisfy this issue**;
      positive-path-only coverage is the review finding `CLAUDE.md` section 5 names as the most common.
- [ ] Exception: the Tamil read is done by somebody who reads Tamil, and every correction is filed against the owning
      sibling with the exact message id. If nobody available reads Tamil, that is recorded as an **open** item with an
      owner and a date — not as a pass, and not as a waiver.
- [ ] Exception: where #52 has merged and the Playwright suite exists, the axe run and the network-failure step are
      recorded as **automated** and this issue records only the device and UAT halves. Say which applied.
