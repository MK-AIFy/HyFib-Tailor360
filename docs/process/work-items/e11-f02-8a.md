- [ ] **Steps 3 and 6 of `A11Y-PZ-03`:** a paid order is received and then dispatched from a phone-width layout, the

      without hunting.

      current state and what to do, never as a generic failure and never as a success; and an expired authorisation on
      the dispatch screen says so and offers the receive scan again.
- [ ] **Exception:** offline, both scans show `OfflineBlockedAction` with the exact sentence beside the form and the
      typed input intact; navigating from an offline story to any other story leaves that story online, because
      `useNetworkState` keeps one module-level snapshot per document.

      outcome; asserted in the route tests by inspecting the stub's received headers.
