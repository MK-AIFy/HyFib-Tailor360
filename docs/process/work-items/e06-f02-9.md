Sub-issue of #33. E06-F02-8 shows a reader which garments are late; nothing tells anybody. Plan decision D12 lists
"due-date/SLA evaluation" among the worker's jobs and plan Section 8 (#33) specifies a scheduled pass that "raises
`JobDueSoon`, `JobOverdue`, `PhaseSlaBreached` … exactly once per job × condition, cleared on completion". The worker
has six hosted services today — `src/Hosts/Tailor360.Worker/Jobs/` holds `OutboxDispatcherService`,
`AuditPartitionMaintenanceService`, `CustomerExportPurgeService`, `DocumentRenderService`, `HeartbeatService` and
`DispatchExceptionExpiryService` — and none of them touches Orders. This slice adds the seventh and the three events
it raises.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pass over a branch with one job due in three days, one overdue by two days and one phase past its configured
      SLA raises exactly three events into `orders.outbox_messages`, each with its condition, the branch's
      computed days or hours, and the pinned workflow version.
- [ ] **Exactly once, which is the hard part:** a second pass five minutes later over unchanged data raises **nothing**
      and writes no outbox row. Asserted by counting outbox rows before and after, not by inspecting the mark columns.
- [ ] The mark and the outbox row are written in the same save: a forced failure after the publish and before the
      commit leaves neither, and the next pass raises the event normally.
- [ ] Due and overdue are evaluated in the **branch** timezone, with the boundary asserted at midnight
      `Asia/Kolkata`: a job due today raises nothing at 23:59 IST and raises `JobOverdue` on the next pass after
      00:00 IST, and the same instants expressed in UTC do not change the answer. This is #33's
      "evaluator boundary at midnight `Asia/Kolkata` with no duplicate raise" verification line.
- [ ] **Negative:** with `Workflow:DueSoonWindowDays` unset, no `JobDueSoon` is ever raised, the pass logs the
      unconfigured window once, and overdue and SLA events still work. No window value appears anywhere in the source.
- [ ] **Negative:** two worker instances running the pass simultaneously raise each condition once in total. The
      lease admits one, and the test runs two evaluators against one database.
- [ ] **Exception:** completing the job's phases clears the marks, so a job that is reopened and becomes late again
      raises again; a cancelled job and a closed branch (`IsOpen` false) are never evaluated, and a phase with no SLA
      configured is never evaluated.
- [ ] A phase whose job is `OnHold` does not accrue an SLA breach while the hold stands —
      `docs/prd/workflows/blouse.md` line 322 states the phase clock pauses while a hold is open — and the paused
      interval is excluded using `job_phases.paused_at`/`.resumed_at` rather than by a second rule.

## Permissions and roles

No endpoint, therefore no endpoint permission. The job runs under the worker's own system scope, which
`src/Platform/Tailor360.Platform.Security/Permissions/PlatformPermissions.cs` documents as authorised "by its
`[WorkerJob]` declaration rather than by a branch assignment it does not have", and ARCH-021 requires that
declaration to name the job's permissions and branch scope. **The scope is `Organisation`, not `None`.**
`WorkerBranchScope` is defined in `src/Platform/Tailor360.Platform.Security/Background/WorkerJobAttribute.cs`:
line 62 gives `None` as "the job touches no branch-owned data — it may act in no branch", and line 76 gives
`Organisation` as "the job reaches every branch in the organisation. The principal carries
`admin.organisation.read_all_branches` for that reason". This pass reads every open branch's `garment_jobs` and
`job_phases`, which is branch-owned data in every branch, so `None` would be a false declaration.
`OutboxDispatcherService.cs:26` already declares `Organisation` for the same reason, and
`DocumentRenderService.cs:19` shows the module permission named alongside it.

| Permission | Scope | MFA | Step-up | Reason | Granted to | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `orders.read` | branch, reached organisation-wide by the declaration | no | no | no | the worker's system principal, by declaration and not by a role grant | **Exists** — `docs/security/permission-matrix.md` line 231. Declared as the job's permission so `WorkerJobDescriptor` can state what the pass is allowed to read |
| `admin.organisation.read_all_branches` | organisation | yes | no | no | carried by the system principal because the scope is `Organisation` | **Exists** — `docs/security/permission-matrix.md` line 241, whose own note says a background job declaring organisation branch scope "carries the same key and additionally acts in every branch by declaration, which is a different thing and is bounded by its `[WorkerJob]` permissions" |

The evaluator publishes no route, so
`[WorkerJob("orders.due_and_sla_evaluation", WorkerBranchScope.Organisation, OrdersPermissions.Read)]` is the
whole authorisation statement (ARCH-021).

No authorisation fixture changes, and `tests/Tailor360.IntegrationTests/Authorization/matrix.yaml` is untouched —
which is itself worth asserting, because a worker job that quietly acquired a route would be invisible there.

## Migration and rollback

One expand-only migration, `33_due_and_sla_marks`: three nullable `timestamptz` columns on two existing tables, no
constraint, no backfill. The previous release tolerates it completely — a nullable column nothing reads is invisible
to it — and rollback is redeploying the previous tag, after which the marks stop being written and the columns are
inert. `Down` drops the three columns and has been executed once. Per `docs/dev/migrations.md` rule 2 and
`src/Modules/CLAUDE.md` section 6. Generated against the head carrying `33_work_queue_indexes`.
