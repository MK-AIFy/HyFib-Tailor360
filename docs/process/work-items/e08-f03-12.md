## Scope

- [ ] **Mount #36's `ScannerSource` on the count sheet in continuous mode**, exactly as #36 configures it for a
      stocktake count — do not re-implement a decode loop, a viewfinder, a debounce, a torch control or a result
      region, all of which are #36's. Each decode resolves the scanned item through whatever resolve path #36 uses and
      **focuses that item's count field**; it does not submit a count on its own, because a count is a quantity a
      person reads off a shelf and the scan only says *which line*.
- [ ] **Focus discipline, which is the real work here.** The count sheet is full of numeric inputs and #36 mounts its
      wedge listener in the app shell "whenever no text input has focus". Decide and write down what happens when a
      wedge scan arrives while a count field has focus — the answer that keeps the clerk's data intact is that the
      sheet does not consume it as text and the scan still selects a line — and assert it with a test that dispatches
      synthetic key events while a field is focused. A wedge scan typed into a quantity box is the defect this child
      exists to prevent.

## What this child cannot prove, and where that evidence lives

A camera decode needs a camera; a keyboard-wedge scan needs a hardware scanner; a 50 × 30 mm thermal label needs a
thermal printer. None of the three exists in a coding session's environment, and **no acceptance criterion below
depends on one**. The hardware evidence is recorded elsewhere and is named here so nobody waits for it:

| Evidence | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| A camera decode on a real device, with permission handling, torch and zoom | **OD-07** (device, browser and printer matrix, open, needed before the W0 exit gate) and **#52** (cross-browser and accessibility gates). #36's own PWA evidence covers the scanner itself |
| A hardware keyboard-wedge scan from a real scanner | #36's evidence, and the printed-label evidence child `E07-F01-9` under #35 |
| Scan round-trip latency against the published budget (`docs/nfr/capacity-and-performance.md`: p95 < 1 s on Blink, < 1.5 s on WebKit, line 218) | #36 and #52. **Not measured here**, because this child adds no network call |
| The automated reflow check at 320 … 1280 px | #52. `clients/pwa/src/design-system/testing/expectNoHorizontalOverflow.ts` takes a Playwright `PageLike`, `tests/e2e` and `playwright.config` do not exist, and no screen test in the tree calls it |

What this child **does** prove, in jsdom: that a decode selects the right line, that an unknown decode is refused in
words, and that a synthetic wedge keystroke does not land in a quantity field. Those are the three things that can be
got wrong in code.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A decode of an item that is **on** the sheet selects that line and places focus in its first-count field, with
      the selection announced in an `aria-live` region — asserted through a stubbed `ScannerSource`, not a real camera.
- [ ] A decode of an item that is **not** on the sheet is refused in words that say so and name what to do next
      (pick from the list, or check the location), and changes no field. **Negative case, and the one a shop will meet
      most often**, because a clerk scanning a shelf will scan something stored in the wrong place.
- [ ] **Negative:** a wedge keystroke sequence dispatched while a count field has focus does **not** appear as text in
      that field, and still selects the scanned line — asserted with synthetic `keydown` events, which is the one part
      of wedge behaviour jsdom can judge.
- [ ] **Negative:** no scan submits a count on its own. A test asserts that a decode produces **zero** requests to the
      counts endpoint; only the person pressing the count action sends one.
- [ ] **Exception:** with no `ScannerSource` available — no camera, permission denied, or #36's source reporting
      unavailable — the sheet renders its manual list and filter unchanged and says the scanner is unavailable. The
      screen is never unusable because a camera is.
- [ ] Accessibility: `expectNoAccessibilityViolations` on the sheet with the scan affordance present and with it
      absent; the scan result is announced; the affordance is keyboard-reachable and is not the only way to select a
      line.
- [ ] The reserved slot `E08-F03-9` left is filled **without moving any existing control**, proved by a screenshot
      diff of the sheet at 320 px before and after.
