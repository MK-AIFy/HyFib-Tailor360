Sub-issue of #37. E07-F03-7 gives a person the screen for **one** garment they already have in their hand. The
parent's sixth implementation step is the other half — "Show pending transfers, overdue acceptance, custody
mismatch, duplicate/stale scans and unknown-location exceptions", each row opening or linking a case — of which
this slice builds the **pending and overdue transfer** half. `docs/nfr/a11y-checklist.md` section 6.4 step 6 —
"As the destination, find the pending transfer in the queue" — is the one step of `A11Y-PZ-02` that E07-F03-7 cannot
answer, because there is no queue, and it is also the step that answers **A11Y-11** ("reachable by at least two
routes") for the job custody screen. Nothing of this exists: `clients/pwa/src/routes/` has no `custody` directory
before E07-F03-7, `grep -rn "custody" clients/pwa/src/app/router.tsx` returns nothing, and
`clients/pwa/src/components/layout/roleNavigation.ts` has no custody destination.
**This slice is the transfer queue and the one navigation destination, and nothing else.** The exception queue, the
case detail, the correction form and the approval are **E07-F03-8b**: by the repository's own calibration a single
non-trivial route is most of a session (`clients/pwa/src/routes/catalog/CatalogVersionEditorRoute.tsx` is 978 lines
for one route, and Billing's `DispatchExceptionApprovalRoute.tsx` is 344 for an approval screen alone), so three
routes in one pull request is two sessions pretending to be one.
