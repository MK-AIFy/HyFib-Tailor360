Sub-issue of #37. The parent's sixth implementation step asks for "custody mismatch, duplicate/stale scans and
unknown-location exceptions" screens and its seventh for "supervised reconciliation/correction using compensating
events — never edit/delete history". E07-F03-8 built the transfer half of step 6; this slice is the exception half
and the whole of step 7. `docs/prd/exceptions.md` **EX-13** (line 50) names the six case types and says each gets
"its own screen"; `docs/nfr/a11y-checklist.md` section 6.4 **step 8** of `A11Y-PZ-02` — "Reject a transfer with a
reason, and **hear the reconciliation case open**" — is the step no earlier child can answer, because only now does
the case exist in front of a person. Nothing of this exists: there is no `clients/pwa/src/routes/custody/` before
E07-F03-7 and `grep -rn "custody/cases" clients/pwa/src/` returns nothing.

**One interpretation, stated rather than assumed.** EX-13 says the six types each get "its own screen". This slice
builds **one queue filtered by type** plus **one case detail**, because the six cases differ in their reason and not
in their repair, the filter gives each type its own address (`/custody/exceptions?type=mismatch`), and six
near-identical routes would be six places for the correction form to drift. Say so in the pull request; if the
reviewer reads EX-13 the other way, the split is six thin wrappers over the same list component and the estimate
does not change much. Do not quietly claim compliance either way.
