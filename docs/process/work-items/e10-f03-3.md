## Scope

- [ ] **Four read routes**, all `GET`, all under the existing `/api/v1/reporting` group, all
      `.RequirePermission(ReportingPermissions.Read, BranchScope.AssignedBranches)` — cross-branch analytics reach
      every branch the caller is assigned to and no further (`BranchScope` doc comment; `CurrentBranch` would make the
      cross-branch report impossible and `Organisation` would grant more than the matrix's `branch` scope allows) —
      each `.RequireRateLimiting(RateLimitPolicyNames.DefaultUser)` and
      `.WithRequestTimeout(RequestTimeoutPolicies.Report)`: `/stock/position` (on-hand, available, reserved,
      in-transit and aging buckets at a cut-off), `/stock/movements` (purchase and consumption quantities with their
      values — the other movement kinds arrive with `e10-f03-2b`, which also adds `/stock/wastage`; do **not** declare
      a column here for data no projection holds), `/stock/variance` (stocktake variance), and `/stock/trends` (any of
      the above metrics bucketed by day, week or month in the branch's timezone). None takes a route parameter, so
      ARCH-023 does not apply — `EndpointAuthorisationInspector` exempts a route whose `RoutePattern.Parameters.Count
      == 0`, and the same inspector exempts an organisation-scoped permission. Say so in the matrix row rather than
      declaring a resource scope that names nothing.
- [ ] **Filters** are server-evaluated and branch-scope-checked: branch, location, item, category key, service-type
      key, movement kind, reason code, cut-off and range. A filter naming a branch the caller is not assigned to is

## Out of scope

The projections themselves — `e10-f03-1`, `e10-f03-2`. Profitability routes and the margin columns — `e10-f03-6`,
which extends `ReportingResponseViews` rather than redefining the surface. Exports of these reports and the export
column policy — `e10-f03-7`. The row-count cap, the queue and cancellation — `e10-f03-8`. Screens —
`e10-f03-9a`/`e10-f03-9b`. The wastage report, its `/stock/wastage` route and its projection — `e10-f03-2b`, which is
blocked on an additive Inventory movement event and adds the route alongside the projection for that reason.

Drill-through **rows**. This is a deliberate, recorded reduction of the parent's fourth implementation step, and the
pull request must say so in one line: the plan's #46 blueprint asks that "every figure drills through to the ledger
entries, purchase receipts, stocktake adjustments or invoices behind it **via `Inventory.Contracts` /
`Billing.Contracts` queries that re-check the caller's branch and permission**" — and neither contract publishes such
a query. `Inventory.Contracts` publishes only `IStockBalanceQuery` and `IValuationQuery`, and
`Billing.Contracts.Payments.IFinancialTotalsQuery` answers balances, not documents (module-ownership sections 5.7 and
5.8). So these payloads carry the source references (`ledger_entry_id`, `purchase_receipt_id`, `stocktake_id`,
`garment_job_id`, `invoice_id`) and the **client navigates to the owning module's own authorised route**. Adding a
ledger-entry or receipt read contract is **additive work on #39/#40**, raised as a follow-up issue in this pull
request, never invented here and never replaced by a cross-schema read (ARCH-005).
