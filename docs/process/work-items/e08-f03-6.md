## Scope

- [ ] **`StockOnHandQuery`** in `…Inventory.Application/Reports/` — for the caller's branch at an optional cut-off
      instant: per `(item, location)` the on-hand, reserved, available and in-transit quantities in the item's base
      unit, with item and location codes, filterable by location and by item, cursor-paged, sorted. No cost column
      and no valuation (field-level minimisation, section 10).
- [ ] **`LowStockQuery`** — the branch's low-stock and replenishment list: every `(item, location)` with an open
      alert from `E08-F03-2`, joined to its reorder rule (#196) and its open purchase quantity, with the shortfall and
      the suggested replenishment quantity (target minus cover) per line. The **suggestion is arithmetic from the
      rule**, not a new policy: no lead-time or safety-stock number is invented here.
- [ ] **The reconciliation footer, computed not copied.** Both reports answer a footer object holding the ledger sum
      at the cut-off and the balance sum at the same cut-off, and a boolean saying whether they agree. A mismatch is
      **reported, never silently corrected** (`docs/nfr/traceability.md` **NFR-BG-06**), and the footer says which
      side is which so a reader can act on it.
- [ ] **The freshness line.** Both reports answer the instant the data was read and, for the low-stock list, the
      instant of the last completed evaluation pass, because alert state is deliberately eventually consistent
      (`docs/architecture/invariants.md` line 395). #44's status strip does not exist yet and is not a dependency;
      this child answers its own two fields and the client renders them (`E08-F03-11`). The last-evaluation instant
      comes from the `inventory.low_stock_evaluation_state` row `E08-F03-2` creates; do not add a second source for it.
- [ ] **Four endpoints.** The two reads are
      `GET /api/v1/inventory/reports/stock-on-hand` and `GET /api/v1/inventory/reports/low-stock`, both

      "Stock, valuation and profitability exports under **`reports.export`**, marked Confidential in the export

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An Inventory Clerk reads the stock-on-hand report for their branch at a cut-off and the figures equal a
      straight sum of the ledger entries up to that instant — asserted over HTTP against a seeded ledger, with the
      footer's two totals agreeing (#40 acceptance criterion 4: "report balances reconcile to ledger at the same
      cutoff time").
- [ ] An Auditor holding `inventory.view_reports` **and `reports.export`** (both are granted to `auditor`) downloads
      both CSV exports with a second factor satisfied and a reason supplied; each file opens with its marked header
      block naming the class, branch, user, instant and report code, and every download is on the audit trail as
      `inventory.report.exported`.
- [ ] **Negative (the consequence of the export key, made visible):** an Inventory Clerk holding
      `inventory.view_reports` but not `reports.export` reads both reports on screen and is refused both downloads
      with `matrix.yaml`'s `not-permitted` answer (`403`, `security.forbidden`); a caller holding `reports.export`
      with a stale second factor is answered `second-factor` (`403`, `security.second-factor-required`).
- [ ] **Negative:** a caller at another branch sees none of this branch's rows, at either report and at either
      export; the branch is never taken from the request. A caller without `inventory.view_reports` is refused
      `matrix.yaml`'s `not-permitted` answer — `403` with `security.forbidden`.
- [ ] **Negative:** an export whose filter would return more rows than the documented cap is refused with a problem
      detail telling the reader to narrow the filter — not truncated silently.
- [ ] **Negative:** a cell whose value begins `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab or carriage return is prefixed and quoted in the
      CSV — asserted by an injection-corpus test over both export paths (**NFR-FI-07**).
- [ ] **Exception:** when the ledger sum and the balance sum disagree at the cut-off, both reports still answer, the
      footer says they disagree and by how much, and nothing is corrected or hidden (**NFR-BG-06**).
- [ ] Both reports answer inside the published budget — **p95 < 1.5 s** (`docs/nfr/capacity-and-performance.md`,
      "Report and read-model queries") — against a ledger seeded to the recorded steady-state size of 110,000
      entries, measured in the test and pasted into the pull request.
