## Scope

- [ ] **`inventory.reservations`** — `id` (UUIDv7), `organisation_id`, `branch_id`, `item_id`, `location_id`,
      `quantity_base numeric(18,4)` positive, `garment_job_id` and `order_id` nullable with no foreign key (Orders
      owns them; ARCH-005), `status` stored as a name in `character varying(20)` — `Held`, `Released`, `Consumed` —
      `held_at`, `resolved_at`, `resolution_reason_code`, `resolved_by_user_id`, `client_request_key`,
      `held_ledger_entry_id` and `resolving_ledger_entry_id`. One migration, `StockReservations`.
- [ ] **The state machine** — `Held → Released` (explicitly, or on a cancelled job) and `Held → Consumed`. The
      `Consumed` transition is driven by **the `Issue` that names the reservation, and by nothing else** — not by a
      `Consumption` entry, which `E08-F02-1`'s fold gives no balance effect and no reservation effect at all. That
      rule is fixed in `E08-F02-1`; this child implements the transition and `E08-F02-3` calls it from its `issue`
      branch. **An issue resolves the reservation in full whatever it issues** — `E08-F02-1`'s fold releases the whole
      held quantity from `reserved`, not the quantity issued — so there is **no partial state, no residual hold and no
      second draw-down**, and `Held`, `Released` and `Consumed` are the whole machine. A resolved reservation never
      returns to `Held`, and a second resolution of the same reservation is a refusal, not a second ledger entry.
      INV-STK-05's first half: a reservation is always resolved — released or converted to consumption, never silently
      dropped.
- [ ] **`POST /api/v1/inventory/reservations`** — body: item, location, quantity with its unit, optional garment job
      and order, optional reason code. Posts one `Reservation` entry through `ILedgerPoster`, which locks the `(item,
      location)` balance row and refuses when `available` (`on_hand - reserved`) is less than the requested base
      quantity, with `inventory.insufficient-stock` and the figure **not** in the message
      (`docs/nfr/data-classification.md` section 5.9). `RequirePermission("inventory.record_movement",
      BranchScope.CurrentBranch)`, `RateLimitPolicyNames.Write`, `.Audited("inventory.reservation.held",
      reasonRequired: true)` (ARCH-008), `.RequireIdempotency()`, `.TouchesNoBranchOwnedResource(...)` with the reason
      and this issue number (ARCH-023).
- [ ] **`POST /api/v1/inventory/reservations/{reservationId}/release`** — body: reason code. Posts one `Release` entry
      and resolves the row. `inventory.record_movement`, `Write`, `.Audited("inventory.reservation.released",
      reasonRequired: true)`, `.RequireIdempotency()`, `.ScopedToResource(InventoryResourceKinds.Reservation,
      "reservationId")`. **`reasonRequired: true` on both commands, because the permission is declared
      `RequiresReason: true`**: `tests/Tailor360.IntegrationTests/Authorization/AuthorisationProbeApplication.cs:721`
      pairs the two from the catalogue, and `tests/Tailor360.UnitTests/Security/PermissionMatrixDocumentTests.cs:84`
      asserts the matrix's Reason column equals the catalogue flag. The flag is metadata only: the reason code arrives
      on the body, and the handler both validates it and writes it onto the audit entry through
      `IInventoryAuditWriter`, because no filter does either (`AuditedEndpointMetadata.cs:5-6` — the filter is #21's,
      and #21 is open).
- [ ] **`GET /api/v1/inventory/reservations/{reservationId}`** — one reservation with its status, its quantities and
      the two ledger entry identifiers. `inventory.record_movement` (the same key the people who create them hold,
      exactly as `GET /api/v1/billing/payments/{paymentId}` reads under `payments.record`),
      `RateLimitPolicyNames.DefaultUser`, `RequestTimeoutPolicies.Read`, `.ScopedToResource(...)`. Another branch's
      reservation reads as **404, not 403**.
- [ ] **`InventoryResourceKinds`** with `Reservation = "inventory.reservation"`, and `ReservationScopeResolver :

- [ ] **`IInventoryEventPublisher`**, its `InventoryDbContext`-bound implementation, and two events in

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An Inventory Clerk with `inventory.record_movement` reserves stock over HTTP and the response carries the
      reservation, its base quantity and `Held`; the item's balance shows the quantity under `reserved`, with
      `on_hand` unchanged and `on_hand - reserved` fallen by it. **The balance is asserted against
      `inventory.balances` through the `InventoryDbContext` in the test, not over HTTP** — this child publishes no
      balance read, and the one that does is `E08-F02-7`, which must not become a dependency of this child's own
      criteria.
- [ ] **Concurrency:** twenty concurrent reservations for a balance that can satisfy five end with exactly five `Held`
      rows, fifteen refusals carrying `inventory.insufficient-stock`, and a `reserved` figure equal to the five
      quantities — the parent's second acceptance criterion, proved against real PostgreSQL and not with a mocked
      store.
- [ ] Every route this child adds declares exactly one permission and one rate-limit policy, the two commands carry
      the audit filter and `Idempotency-Key`, and each `{reservationId}` route declares a resource scope (ARCH-007,
      ARCH-008, ARCH-017, ARCH-023). `./scripts/dev test contract` passes with the regenerated document committed.
- [ ] **Negative**, with every wire answer taken from the `answers` block of
      `tests/Tailor360.IntegrationTests/Authorization/matrix.yaml` rather than invented: a caller without
      `inventory.record_movement` gets `not-permitted` — 403 `security.forbidden` — and the attempt is audited; a
      caller from another branch reading or releasing a reservation gets `out-of-reach` — 404
      `security.resource-not-found`; a replayed `Idempotency-Key` returns the first outcome with
      `Idempotency-Replayed` and creates no second ledger entry; the same key with a different body is
      `idempotency.key-reused` (422, `IdempotencyProblems.KeyReused`).
- [ ] **Negative:** releasing an already-released reservation is refused with a stable
      `inventory.reservation-already-resolved` code and posts no `Release` entry; the first release's entry is still
      the only one.
- [ ] **Exception:** `orders.job-cancelled.v1` delivered for a job holding two reservations releases both, writes two
      `Release` entries, and a redelivery of the same message releases nothing further and writes no further entry —
      the inbox row is what makes that true.
- [ ] A reservation refused for insufficient stock writes **no** `Reservation` ledger entry and leaves `reserved`
      unchanged.
- [ ] `inventory.stock-reserved.v1` and `inventory.stock-released.v1` both appear in `docs/integration/events/` with a
      schema and an example, pass the wire-name grammar, and carry no unit cost, no customer identifier and no free
      text.
