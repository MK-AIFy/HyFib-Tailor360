Sub-issue of #33. Plan Section 8 (#33) lists a "'Team' screen for capabilities" among this feature's screens, and it
is the only one of the four with no prototype and no route: `grep -rn "capabilit" clients/pwa/src` returns nothing, and
`clients/pwa/src/components/layout/roleNavigation.ts` has no team destination. E06-F02-6 publishes the three capability
routes and E06-F02-7 publishes the workload read, and nothing reaches either. Without this screen a Tailor Master can
be refused an assignment for "no capability for this category and phase" with no way to see or fix the capability —
which is `A11Y-RJ-04` step 4 leaving the person stuck.

## Out of scope

The capability tables and routes themselves (E06-F02-6) and the assignment routes and workload read (E06-F02-7). Teams
as assignees: there is no team anywhere in this solution and E06-F02-6 records the question rather than inventing one,
so this screen lists **people**. Enforcing capacity — it is displayed, never a limit, because no document says what
happens when it is exceeded. Editing a person's roles, which is `/admin/users` and #25's. Throughput and on-time rates,
which are `assigneeThroughput` on the server's view, gated on `reporting.read`, and belong to #45's analytics; this
screen shows the open-phase count and the stated capacity and nothing derived from them. The workboard and planning
board (E06-F02-11). Any new navigation destination.

## Data and classification

This screen is almost entirely Personal data about staff, and section 5.15 is the authority on all of it.

| Item | Class | Purpose | Retention | Who may read it | Logs, telemetry, export |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Staff display name | Personal (`docs/nfr/data-classification.md` 5.15) | Name the person a capability is about | Not stored on the device | `orders.assign` holders in the branch; `admin.users` for administration; the individual for their own profile | **Never** in a log, a metric, a trace attribute or client telemetry |
| Capability rows: category code, phase code, valid-from, valid-to, grant and revocation reasons | Personal in effect (5.15 names "assignee capabilities") with Internal codes (5.7) | Say what somebody may be assigned | Not stored | As above | Codes are safe server-side; the person they are about is not |
| Stated daily capacity | Personal (5.15) — a statement about one person's working day | Assign against capacity | Not stored | **Tailor Master, Branch Manager, Owner only** (5.15) | Never a metric dimension keyed by person |
| Open-phase count per person | Personal (5.15) | See who is loaded | Not stored | As above | Never in client telemetry |
| Throughput / on-time rate | Staff performance | — | — | **Not shown here at all.** `reporting.read` gates it on the server and #45 owns the surface that shows it | — |
| `localStorage` | — | **Nothing is stored.** No staff name, no capability and no filter is written to browser storage | — | — | — |

A shared tablet left on this screen is the wall display DC-11 forbids, which is why the route is gated on
`orders.assign` rather than on `orders.read`, and why nothing is cached locally.

| Screen | Route | Loading | Empty | Error | Offline | Forbidden |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

`admin.forbidden.*`; `states.*`; `dialogs.*`. Dates go through `clients/pwa/src/i18n/formatters.ts`; there is no money
and no measurement. The screen joins the Tamil catalogue and is re-checked at 40% text growth, where the
category-and-phase pair labels are the thing most likely to wrap badly.

**No offline story and no offline screenshot**, for the reason
`clients/pwa/src/routes/catalog/catalogScreens.stories.tsx` records for every administrative screen: granting a
capability is an online-only act, the application says so through `OfflineBlockedAction`, and an offline story would
imply a queue this screen must not have. Loading, empty, error and forbidden stories are required and each renders the
real route through `withAdminApi`.
