## The offline story, because this is where a reviewer and the tree disagree

`docs/process/definition-of-done.md` **DoD 7** asks, word for word, that "Storybook stories exist for the loading,
empty, error, **offline** and forbidden state of every new screen", and `clients/pwa/CLAUDE.md` section 6 repeats it.
But `clients/pwa/src/routes/catalog/catalogScreens.stories.tsx` ships **no** offline story and records why in its own
doc comment: "editing and publishing a catalogue are online-only acts, and the application says so through
`NetworkStatusBanner` and `OfflineBlockedAction`, which carry their own stories. An offline story here would imply
these screens queue what a person did, and they must not."

Read that exception for what it is: it is about a screen whose offline story would **imply queueing**. These are
**operational** screens whose offline state is a *visible refusal* — `OfflineBlockedAction` saying the action will not
be queued — which is exactly what **NFR-CU-05** requires of inventory actions and what a person on a shop floor needs
to see. So: **write the offline story**, and put one sentence in the story file's doc comment saying that it renders
the refusal rather than a queue, so it reads as following the catalogue's reasoning rather than ignoring it. Do **not**
produce an offline *screenshot* at five widths; the offline state is a story and a Vitest assertion, not a device
photograph.

      lands on the count sheet, records a first count for three lines, records a recount with a reason for one line,

      `Idempotency-Key` held across a retry.

      wording rule.
- [ ] **Negative:** the first count field is not editable after it has been recorded, and the screen says why; a
      caller without `inventory.stocktake` sees the `RequirePermission` empty state (`admin.forbidden.title` /
      `admin.forbidden.body`) and is not redirected.
- [ ] **Negative:** a `409` from opening a second session for a location, and a `409` from counting after counting is
      closed, each render the server's own words through `plainLanguageDetail`, and the `Idempotency-Key` is unchanged
      on the retry.
- [ ] **Exception/offline:** with the network down the sheet still shows its last read value and every write renders
      `OfflineBlockedAction` saying it will not be queued — `A11Y-RJ-05` step 9.
- [ ] Accessibility: `expectNoAccessibilityViolations` on every state of both screens; the two count fields are
      distinguishable to a screen reader by label alone (`A11Y-RJ-05` step 5: "hearing the two fields apart"); the
      frozen location is announced on arrival (step 4); every control is keyboard-operable in reading order.
